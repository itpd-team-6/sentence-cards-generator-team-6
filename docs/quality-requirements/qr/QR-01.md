# QR-01: A Word Without A Sentence Still Becomes A Card The Learner Can Retry

When the language model gives no sentence for a marked word, the learner still gets a card for that word and can ask for its sentence again, and the other words keep their sentences.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Reliability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Fault Tolerance
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

The external language model, called by the `backend` through the endpoint set in `.env`.

### Stimulus

The model fails to return a usable sentence for one marked word, after the request has been repeated up to the limit of [`DEC-024`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#dec-024) (5 requests in all, with a growing wait).

### Environment

A learner generates the cards of a text that has several marked words, and the model answers for some words and fails for at least one, for example because a free plan is overloaded.

### Artifact

The card generation in `backend/app/routes/cards.py` and the cards store in `backend/app/database.py`.

### Response

The `backend` shall create a card for the failed word with no sentence and an error on it, keep the sentences of the words that succeeded, and let the learner ask for the failed word's sentence again, which then fills the same card.

### Response Measure

For a text whose marked words all get cards, a word the model failed has exactly one card with an empty sentence and a non-empty error, every word that succeeded keeps its sentence, and a later generate call for that text adds no second card for the failed word but fills its sentence when the model answers.
