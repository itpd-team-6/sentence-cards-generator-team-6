# QR-08: A New Learner Gets From A Newly Opened Library To The First Answered Card Without Help

A learner who has not used the product before and is not on the team gets from a newly opened library to the first answered card without help, and rates that task at least 5 of 7 for ease.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Self-descriptiveness
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

A learner of Russian, English or German who has not used the product before, is 18 or older, and is not a member of the team, the Customer or the course staff.

### Stimulus

The learner wants to remember some words of a short text they read in the language they are learning, and is asked to get ready to practise those words as cards and to answer the first card.

### Environment

The staging deployment on the university network, with a library that has just been opened and holds no texts and no cards, and a facilitator who answers only questions about the scenario and never about the interface.

### Artifact

The pages of the `frontend` that lead from a newly opened library to the first answered card, and the `backend` requests behind them.

### Response

The learner reaches the first answered card without being told which page or control to use, and finds the task easy.

### Response Measure

In at least three counted sessions of the usability test `UT-01` the median score of the main usability test task on the Single Ease Question (SEQ), where 1 is very difficult and 7 is very easy, is at least 5 of 7, reported with the number of sessions.
The usability test measures self-descriptiveness because the product has no tutorial, so the learner has to recognise from the pages alone what to do next, and the SEQ is the learner's own rating of how easy that was.
The other recorded measures, which are the completion and assistance, the time on task and the System Usability Scale (SUS) score, stay results of the test and are reported, and the requirement reads only the median SEQ of the main task.

## QRT-01

A reviewer checks that the median SEQ of the main usability test task of the usability test `UT-01` is at least 5 of 7 in at least three counted sessions.

- **Status:** Planned
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** the usability test `UT-01`, which runs at least three counted sessions on the staging deployment, and the reading of its `results.md`.
- **Why Manual:** no command can judge how easy a person finds a task, because the measure is the participant's own SEQ answer, and the test reads no `SLI-nn`.

### Procedure

1. Run the main usability test task of `UT-01` with at least three counted participants, one session each, as the protocol of the `UT-01` record says.
2. After the main task, ask each participant the SEQ as `surveys.md` of `UT-01` words it, and record the answer in the session note.
3. Open `results.md` of `UT-01` and read the median SEQ of the main task and the number of counted sessions.

**Pass when:** the median SEQ of the main task in `results.md` is at least 5 of 7, with at least three counted sessions.
