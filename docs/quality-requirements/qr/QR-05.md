# QR-05: The Cards Show Within Half A Second After The Model's Answer Arrives

After the answer of the language model has arrived, the product shows the cards of that answer within half a second, so that the only wait the learner has is the one for the model.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Performance Efficiency
- **Sub-characteristic:** Time Behaviour
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Low
- **Changed:**
  - The outcome was that the time spent repeating a request for one word stays within about nineteen seconds. The Customer said that this time "does not depend on you. It depends on this API", and that what can be measured is the time to display the cards after the response has arrived ([`DEC-039`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/blob/main/docs/decisions.md#dec-039)). The outcome and the measure are now the time to show the cards.
  - The limit of half a second is the team's choice, from the Customer's example. The wait of the repeated requests is not a quality requirement any more; it stays in [`FR-002`](../../functional-requirements/fr/FR-002.md).

## Scenario

### Source

The language model service, whose answer for the marked words of a text reaches the product.

### Stimulus

The answer of the model for the marked words of a text arrives at the `backend`.

### Environment

A learner has marked up to 20 words of a text, and the product runs on a local machine with the cards table of the text open.

### Artifact

The `backend` that saves the cards of the answer, in `backend/app/routes/cards.py`, and the cards table that the `frontend` shows, in `frontend/src/CardsTable.jsx`.

### Response

The product shall save the cards of the answer and show them in the cards table without any wait for the model.

### Response Measure

From the moment the answer of the model has arrived to the moment all its cards are visible in the cards table, at most 0.5 seconds in at least 9 of 10 runs for a text with up to 20 marked words; the time the model takes to answer is not part of the measure.
