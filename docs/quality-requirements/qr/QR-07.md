# QR-07: An Operator Finds A Failed Request And Its Latency From The Signals

When a request of the core task fails, an operator finds the failed request and reads how slow the failures are from the product's signals alone, without reading the server's files.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Maintainability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Analysability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

The operator of the product, a member of the team who looks after the deployment.

### Stimulus

A request of the core task fails, such as the request for the cards of a text that does not exist.

### Environment

The product is deployed in staging, collection is on, and the operator reaches OpenObserve from the university network.

### Artifact

The `backend` and its signals: the log line of each request and the histogram of request durations, as [`SLI-01`](../../observability.md#sli-01) defines them.

### Response

The product shall keep a log point for the failed request, with its route and its failure, and shall keep the failure series of the request duration apart from the series of the successful requests.

### Response Measure

Within five seconds of the failed request's response, a query of the staging organization returns one log point for it, and within one minute, the query of [`SLI-01`](../../observability.md#sli-01) returns the failure series with a P95 value.

## QRT-01

A script sends test traffic to the staging deployment, makes one request fail, and checks that the log point and the failure series of the P95 appear in the time the measure sets.

- **Status:** Implemented
- **Level:** deployed
- **Runs at:** the staging deployment at `$STAGING_URL`, read in OpenObserve at `$OPENOBSERVE_URL` through the query of [`SLI-01`](../../observability.md#sli-01), run by a person from a machine on the university network with `node scripts/check-sli.mjs`; the address of the organization and the credential come from the environment and are not in this repository.

### Procedure

Set `STAGING_URL`, `OPENOBSERVE_URL`, `OPENOBSERVE_ORG`, `OPENOBSERVE_USER` and `OPENOBSERVE_PASSWORD` in the environment, and run [`scripts/check-sli.mjs`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/blob/main/scripts/check-sli.mjs).
It sends two successful requests and one request for the cards of a text that does not exist, which answers 404 and the outcome `client_error`, all tagged `source=smoke` and with a library of their own.
It then reads the `X-Request-ID` of the failed response, queries the log stream `backend_requests` of the staging organization for that identifier, and runs the query of [`SLI-01`](../../observability.md#sli-01).

**Pass when:** in the staging deployment, the query of the log stream returns exactly one log point for the failed request, with the route `/api/texts/{text_id}/cards` and the outcome `client_error`, within five seconds of the response, and the query of `SLI-01` returns the series of that route with the outcome `client_error` and a numeric P95, within one minute of the response.
