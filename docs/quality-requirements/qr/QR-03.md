# QR-03: A Person Outside The Team Installs And Runs The Product From docs/setup.md Alone

A person who is not on the team installs and runs the product by following only `docs/setup.md`, with no other help from the team.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Flexibility
- **Sub-characteristic:** Installability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

A person outside the team, such as the Customer or a self-hoster, who wants to run the product.

### Stimulus

The person installs and starts the product, following only the steps in `docs/setup.md`.

### Environment

The person's own machine or a VPS, as [`CON-07`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/blob/main/docs/product-vision.md#con-07) requires, with no access to the team and no instructions other than the file.

### Artifact

`docs/setup.md` and the backend and frontend it tells the person to install and run.

### Response

The person shall reach a running product, with the backend serving the built frontend, using only the steps in the file.

### Response Measure

Following only `docs/setup.md`, a person outside the team reaches the running product and opens its page, with no step that the file leaves out, which is the state [`TOS-02`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/blob/main/docs/threshold-of-success.md#tos-02) checks.
