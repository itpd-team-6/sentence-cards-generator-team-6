# FR-013: Put The Cards Of A Text In The Decks The Learner Chooses

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-11`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/201), [`DEC-020`](../../decisions.md#dec-020), [`DEC-047`](../../decisions.md#dec-047)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner asks for the cards of a text, the product shall put every card of the text in all the decks the learner chose, and shall ask for at least one deck.

When no deck is chosen, or a chosen deck does not exist or is for another pair of languages than the text, the product shall refuse the request, make no card and save nothing.

When a word of the text already has a card with the same meaning, as [`FR-009`](FR-009.md) says, the product shall make no second card, shall not ask the language model, and shall put the card that exists in the chosen decks.

When the language model gives no sentence for a word, the product shall put the card without a sentence in the chosen decks too.

The text page shall show only the decks of the pair of languages of the text, with their number of cards, a box to tick the decks the cards go in, and a search box when there are many decks.
The page shall ask for the languages before it shows the decks, and shall say how many decks of other pairs are hidden.

The learner shall be able to make a new deck on the text page, for the pair of languages of the text, and the new deck shall be ticked.
When the product refuses the title, the page shall show the reason and tick nothing.

The text page shall say how many words will become cards and in how many decks.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a text and two decks, when the learner asks for the cards, then each card is in both decks.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_cards_of_a_text_go_in_the_chosen_decks`.

### AC-02

Given no chosen deck, when the learner asks for the cards, then the product refuses and no card is made.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_at_least_one_deck_is_needed_to_ask_for_cards_and_no_card_is_made_without_it`.

### AC-03

Given a deck that does not exist or is for another pair of languages, when the learner asks for the cards, then nothing is made or saved.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_deck_that_does_not_exist_or_is_for_another_pair_stops_everything`.

### AC-04

Given a word that already has a card, when the learner asks for the cards in new decks, then the card is added to the new decks and the language model is not asked.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_word_that_already_has_a_card_is_added_to_the_new_decks_without_asking_the_model` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_word_with_the_same_meaning_in_another_text_is_added_to_the_decks_too`.

### AC-05

Given a word the model gave no sentence for, when the learner asks for the cards, then its card is in the chosen decks.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_card_the_model_gave_no_sentence_for_is_in_the_decks_too`.

### AC-06

Given no language chosen, when the learner opens the deck box of the text page, then it asks for the languages first and shows no deck.

Test: `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::it asks for the languages first`.

### AC-07

Given decks of two pairs of languages, when the learner opens the deck box, then only the decks of the pair show, with their cards, and the box says how many are hidden.

Test: `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::it offers only the decks of the language pair, with their cards, and says how many are hidden`.

### AC-08

Given the deck box, when the learner ticks a deck and unticks it, then it is added to the chosen decks and removed, and a screen reader is told that the deck is ticked.

Test: `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::ticking a deck adds it to the chosen decks, and unticking removes it` and `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::a ticked deck says so to a screen reader`.

### AC-09

Given the deck box, when the learner makes a new deck, then it is made for the pair of languages, it is ticked and the form closes.

Test: `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::a new deck is made for the language pair, ticked, and the form closes`.

### AC-10

Given the form of a new deck, when the title is empty, then the button waits, and when the product refuses a title, then the reason shows and nothing is ticked.

Test: `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::the button to create a deck waits for a title, and Cancel closes the form` and `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::a title the product refuses is shown and nothing is ticked`.

### AC-11

Given many decks, when the learner opens the deck box, then there is a search box.

Test: `frontend/src/DeckPicker.test.jsx::with many decks there is a search box`.
