# FR-001: Make A Card For Each Marked Word

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-01`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/27), [`DEC-005`](../../decisions.md#dec-005)
- **Containers:** `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner asks for the cards of a saved text, the product shall make one card for each marked word of the text that has no card yet, with a sentence that uses the word and the translation of that sentence into the learner's first language.

The product shall give the language model the part of the text around the word.

The product shall make the cards at once, without asking the learner to confirm each one, and shall keep them after a restart.

When the learner asks again for the cards of the same text, the product shall make no second card for a word that has one and shall not change the cards that exist.

When the language model gives no sentence for a word, the product shall keep a card for that word without a sentence and with the reason, and shall keep the sentences of the other words.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a saved text with marked words and a model that answers, when the learner asks for the cards, then each marked word has a card with a sentence and a translation.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_every_marked_word_gets_a_card_with_a_sentence_and_its_translation`.

### AC-02

Given a text whose cards exist, when the learner asks for the cards again, then no card is added and none changes.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_asking_again_does_not_touch_the_cards_that_exist`.

### AC-03

Given a model that fails for one word, when the cards are made, then that card has no sentence and carries a message, and the other cards keep their sentences.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_word_the_model_fails_for_has_no_sentence_and_the_others_keep_theirs`.

### AC-04

Given cards that were just made, when the product restarts, then they are still there without anything having been confirmed.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_cards_exist_without_a_confirmation_and_survive_a_restart`.

### AC-05

Given a marked word, when its card is made, then the request to the model carries the part of the text around the word.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_model_gets_the_part_of_the_text_around_the_word`.
