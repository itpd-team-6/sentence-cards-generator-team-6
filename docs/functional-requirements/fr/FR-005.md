# FR-005: Ask For New Sentences For The Chosen Cards

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/28), [`DEC-016`](../../decisions.md#dec-016), [`DEC-028`](../../decisions.md#dec-028)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner asks for a new sentence for one card, the product shall replace the sentence and the translation of that card only, and shall tell the language model the old sentence so that it gives another.

When the translation of a card is locked, the product shall keep that translation when a new sentence is asked for, and shall ask the language model for a sentence that means the same as it.

When the learner asks for new sentences for several chosen cards, the product shall replace the sentences of the chosen cards only.

When the language model gives nothing for a chosen card, the product shall keep the current sentence of that card, shall report the reason, and shall still give the other chosen cards their new sentences.

When one of the chosen cards does not exist, the product shall stop before it asks the language model.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a card, when the learner asks for a new sentence, then only that card changes and the model is told the old sentence.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_new_sentence_replaces_only_that_card_and_the_model_is_told_the_old_one`.

### AC-02

Given several cards, when the learner asks for new sentences for the chosen ones, then only those sentences are replaced.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_new_sentences_for_the_chosen_cards_replace_only_those_sentences`.

### AC-03

Given a model that fails for one chosen card, when new sentences are asked for, then that card keeps its sentence and the others get new ones.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_card_the_model_fails_for_keeps_its_sentence_and_the_others_get_new_ones`.

### AC-04

Given chosen cards of which one does not exist, when new sentences are asked for, then the model is not asked.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_card_that_does_not_exist_stops_the_new_sentences_before_the_model_is_asked`.

### AC-05

Given ticked cards, when the learner asks for their new sentences, then the cards being worked on show that the product is working and the result is shown for each.

Checked by hand in the browser, because the frontend has no automated test yet.

### AC-06

Given a card whose translation is locked, when a new sentence is asked for, then the sentence is new, the translation does not change, and the model is given the locked translation; a card that is not locked gets a new sentence and a new translation.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_new_sentence_for_a_locked_card_keeps_the_translation_and_the_model_is_given_it`, `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_new_sentences_for_several_cards_keep_the_locked_translations_and_change_the_others` and `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_new_sentence_for_a_card_that_is_not_locked_changes_the_sentence_and_the_translation`.
