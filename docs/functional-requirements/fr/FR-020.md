# FR-020: Find, Search And Organize The Cards In Your Cards

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-17`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/234), [`DEC-067`](../../decisions.md#dec-067)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - The item "Uncategorized" of the filters is shown only when the learner has no deck "Uncategorized", because a card that leaves its last deck now goes to that deck ([`DEC-065`](../../decisions.md#dec-065)), and the opened card shows its decks as links ([`FR-022`](FR-022.md)).
  - A card in the list no longer shows all its decks as labels; it shows the text it came from and the decks the learner ticked ([`DEC-067`](../../decisions.md#dec-067)).

## Behavior

The product shall show all the cards of the learner on the page "Your cards", with a list of filters at the left of the cards, in a box that starts with "All cards" and its number, which is chosen while no filter is ticked and clears the filters when the learner clicks it, and the page of a text shall show only the cards of that text, as [`FR-019`](FR-019.md) says.

The list of filters shall have the decks, with the item "Uncategorized" for the cards that are in no deck, and the texts, with the item "No text" for the cards whose text was deleted, each with a box and the number of cards it gives.
It shall have the language pairs only when the cards have more than one pair, and the statuses: hidden from study, marked for review and without a sentence.

When the learner ticks several items of one part, the product shall list the cards that are in at least one of them, and the learner shall be able to choose "All" to list only the cards that are in every one of them.
The product shall offer this choice only when two or more items of the part are ticked.
The product shall list only the cards that pass every part that has a choice.

The number of an item shall be the number of cards the learner would get by ticking it, with the other choices and the search applied, and an item that gives no card shall be dimmed.
A ticked item shall stay in the list, so that the learner can untick it.

Above the cards, the product shall say in a sentence how many cards are listed and what they have in common, and shall show each ticked item and the search as a chip with a cross, and a "Clear" that takes them all away.

When no card is listed, the product shall say that no card has all the choices and that the learner can clear the filters or change the search.

When exactly one text is ticked, the product shall offer "Open text", which opens the page of that text.

The product shall search the cards by the word, the context phrase, the sentence and the translation, without the case of the letters and without accents, and the list shall show the cards that have what the learner typed, together with the ticked items.
Under the search box, a panel shall say how many cards, texts and decks were found and list them in a section each, three rows at first and "Show more" for the rest, and shall have a choice to look only in cards, texts or decks.
A card of the panel shall put its word in the search, a text or a deck of the panel shall be ticked in the list of filters and clear the search, and the arrow of a text shall open it.
Escape shall close the panel.
Enter shall take what the learner typed even if it is not complete: when cards have it they stay listed, when no card has it the decks whose title has it are ticked, or else the texts, and with the kind of the panel on decks or on texts, those are ticked.

Each card shall be one compact line with the word, the sentence on one line, an icon when the card is marked for review or hidden from study, which is a button that undoes it (a hidden card is dimmed), the title of the first text that has the word, which opens that text, with "+" and the number of the other texts when there are more, and only the decks the learner ticked that the card is in, then a deck icon with the number of decks the card is in (the names of the decks show when the pointer rests on it). The text and the number of decks shall be in columns on the right, so they line up from one line to the next. A click on the line shall open it to show the translation, with its lock, and the buttons to edit the card, mark it for review, hide it from study, ask for a new sentence and delete it.
The box that edits a card shall list the decks of the card.

The learner shall be able to sort the cards with the newest first, which is the first choice, with the oldest first, or from A to Z.
The learner shall be able to group the cards by deck or by text, with a title and the number of cards over each group and a last group for the cards that are in no deck or from no text, and a card that is in two groups shall show in both.
The title of a group shall be a button that closes and opens it, and the page shall have "Collapse all" and "Expand all", which count only the groups on the screen; the groups are open at first, so that closing them all leaves only the list of decks or of texts with their numbers of cards.

The bar of the selected cards shall say how many cards are selected of the cards listed, and "Select all" shall select only the listed cards.
The bar shall have "Decks", which opens a box with the decks of the language pair of the selected cards: a deck is ticked when all of them are in it and dashed when some are, and the learner can put all of them in a deck or take all of them out.
The product shall change only the link between each card and its decks, so that the card keeps its sentence, its translation and its study data, and shall refuse, and change nothing, when a card or a deck does not exist, when a deck is of another language pair than a card, when no deck is sent, or when the same deck is to be added and removed.

The product shall keep the search, the ticked items, the way of grouping and the order in the browser while the tab is open, so that the learner who opens a text from a card and goes back finds the list as it was, and "Clear" shall not change the way of grouping or the order.

On a narrow screen, the product shall show the search box and the button "Filters" one under the other, the button with the number of ticked items, and shall open the list of filters in a sheet from the bottom, with a button that shows how many cards the choices give.
