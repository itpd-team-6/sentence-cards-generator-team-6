# FR-010: Edit The Short Phrase Of A Card

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`DEC-013`](../../decisions.md#dec-013), [`DEC-017`](../../decisions.md#dec-017)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner opens the editor of a card, the product shall show the phrase of the card, with its sentence and its translation, and shall let the learner change it.

When the learner saves a phrase, the product shall keep it with the spaces cleaned up, and shall keep it after a restart.

The product shall refuse a phrase that has fewer than 2 or more than 6 words, or that does not contain the word of the card, and shall change nothing on the card.

The product shall refuse a phrase that another card of the same word has, whatever the case and the spaces, and shall change nothing on the card.

When the product refuses what the learner saved, the editor shall stay open with the reason and with what the learner wrote.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a card, when the learner saves a new phrase, then the card has it, with the spaces cleaned up, and keeps it after a restart.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_learner_edits_the_phrase_of_a_card`.

### AC-02

Given a card, when the learner saves a phrase of one word, or one without the word of the card, then the product refuses and the card does not change.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_phrase_that_cannot_identify_the_card_is_refused_and_nothing_changes` and `backend/tests/test_phrases.py`.

### AC-03

Given two cards of the same word with different phrases, when the learner saves on one of them the phrase of the other, then the product refuses with the phrase of the other card and nothing changes.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_phrase_another_card_of_the_word_has_is_refused_and_nothing_changes`.

### AC-04

Given two cards of different words, when the learner saves the same phrase on both, then the product accepts it, because the phrase and the word together identify the card.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_same_phrase_on_two_different_words_is_not_a_second_card`.

### AC-05

Given a card, when the learner clicks its Edit button, then a window opens with the phrase, the sentence and the translation, the cursor is in the phrase, Escape closes it, and a refused phrase leaves the window open with the reason below the fields.

Checked by hand in the browser, because the frontend has no automated test yet.
