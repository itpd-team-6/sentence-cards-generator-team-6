# FR-009: Identify A Card By Its Word And A Short Phrase

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`DEC-013`](../../decisions.md#dec-013)
- **Containers:** `backend`

## Behavior

When the product makes a card for a word, it shall ask the language model for a context phrase (the "short phrase" of [`DEC-013`](../../decisions.md#dec-013)), of three or four words, that uses the word with the meaning it has in the text, and shall keep that phrase with the card.

The product shall give the language model the phrases of the cards that already exist for the word, and shall tell it to answer that the card exists when the word has the same meaning as one of them.

When the language model answers that the card exists, or writes a phrase that a card of the word already has, the product shall make no card for that word and shall tell the learner which word it is and the phrase of the card that exists.

When the learner marks a word that has a card with another meaning, the product shall make the card, so that a word has one card for each phrase.

When the learner asks for a new sentence for a card that has a phrase, the product shall keep the phrase and shall ask the language model for a sentence in which the word has the meaning of that phrase.

When a card has no sentence and no phrase, because the model did not answer when it was made, the product shall ask for the phrase together with the new sentence, and shall change nothing when the word already has a card with that meaning.

A card made before phrases existed shall have no phrase, shall keep working as before, and shall get a phrase only when the learner writes one.

The database shall not hold two cards that have the same word and the same phrase.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a marked word and a model that answers, when the card is made, then it has a phrase, and the request to the model asked for it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_new_card_gets_the_phrase_that_goes_with_its_word` and `backend/tests/test_llm.py::test_a_card_is_asked_for_a_minimal_phrase_with_the_word_in_its_meaning`.

### AC-02

Given a word that already has a card with a phrase, when a card is asked for the same word in another text, then the request to the model lists that phrase.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_model_is_told_the_phrases_the_word_already_has` and `backend/tests/test_llm.py::test_the_phrases_the_word_already_has_are_listed_and_the_model_may_answer_that_one_exists`.

### AC-03

Given a word that has a card, when the model says the word has the same meaning, then no card is made, the cards do not change, and the answer names the word and the phrase of the card that exists.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_no_card_is_made_when_the_word_already_has_a_card_with_the_same_meaning`.

### AC-04

Given a word that has a card, when the model gives a phrase with another meaning, then a second card is made for the word.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_word_with_a_different_meaning_gets_a_second_card`.

### AC-05

Given a word that has a card, when the model does not say that the card exists but writes the same phrase, whatever the case and the spaces, then no card is made.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_phrase_the_model_repeats_makes_no_second_card_even_when_it_does_not_say_so` and `backend/tests/test_llm.py::test_a_new_phrase_that_is_one_the_word_already_has_raises_card_exists`.

### AC-06

Given a text with two marked words of which one has a card with the same meaning, when the cards are made, then the other word gets its card.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_other_words_of_the_text_still_get_their_cards_when_one_word_exists`.

### AC-07

Given a card with a phrase, when the learner asks for a new sentence, then the card keeps its phrase, and the request to the model carries it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_new_sentence_keeps_the_phrase_and_the_meaning_of_the_word`.

### AC-08

Given a card without a sentence, when the learner asks for its sentence, then the card gets a phrase with it, and when the word already has a card with that meaning, then nothing changes and the learner is told.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_retry_of_a_card_without_a_sentence_also_writes_its_phrase` and `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_retry_of_a_card_without_a_sentence_is_refused_when_the_word_already_has_that_meaning`.

### AC-09

Given a database made before phrases existed, when the product starts, then its cards are kept and have no phrase, and a new sentence for one of them does not ask for a phrase.

Test: `backend/tests/test_database.py::test_a_database_made_before_phrases_keeps_its_cards_and_they_have_no_phrase` and `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_card_made_before_phrases_has_no_phrase_and_the_learner_can_add_one`.

### AC-10

Given a card with a word and a phrase, when a second card with the same word and phrase is saved, then the database refuses it, and cards without a phrase do not count as the same card.

Test: `backend/tests/test_database.py::test_two_cards_cannot_have_the_same_word_and_the_same_phrase` and `backend/tests/test_database.py::test_cards_without_a_phrase_do_not_count_as_the_same_card`.
