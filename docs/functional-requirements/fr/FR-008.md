# FR-008: Order The Study Queue By Due Time And Let The Learner Move Cards

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-04`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/30), [`DEC-021`](../../decisions.md#dec-021), [`DEC-023`](../../decisions.md#dec-023)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

The product shall list every card that has a sentence in the order the cards are studied, by due time, with the cards due today first and the cards due later after them with their dates.

When the learner moves cards, the product shall give only the moved cards a new due time, between the cards next to them, and shall leave the other cards as they were.

When a card is moved from the cards due today to the later cards, or the other way, the product shall change its due date to fit its new place and shall not change its interval.

When the cards of the queue are not the ones the learner saw, or the new order is not valid, the product shall refuse the order and change nothing.

When the learner answers a moved card, the product shall give it the due date that the rule of the scheduler sets and shall leave the other cards in place.

The product shall keep the order after a restart.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given cards due the same day, when they are read, then they are in the order of their due times, and a card due on an earlier day comes first.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_cards_due_the_same_day_are_studied_in_the_order_of_their_due_times` and `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_that_is_due_on_an_earlier_day_comes_before_a_card_due_today`.

### AC-02

Given the cards of two texts, when the learner moves the cards of the second text to the top, then they are studied before the cards of the first.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_cards_of_the_second_text_dragged_to_the_top_are_studied_before_the_first_text`.

### AC-03

Given a card dragged between two others, when the order is saved, then only that card changes its place and its due time.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_dragged_between_two_others_changes_only_its_own_place_and_due_time`.

### AC-04

Given a later card moved among the cards due today, when the order is saved, then it is due today and keeps its interval.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_later_card_dragged_above_the_separator_is_due_today_and_keeps_its_interval`.

### AC-05

Given an order for cards that are no longer in the queue, when it is saved, then the product refuses it and changes nothing.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_an_order_for_cards_that_are_no_longer_in_the_queue_is_refused_and_changes_nothing`.

### AC-06

Given a moved card, when the learner answers it, then it gets the due date of the scheduler and the others stay in place.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_moved_card_that_is_answered_gets_the_due_date_of_the_scheduler_and_the_others_stay_in_place`.

### AC-07

Given a new order, when the product restarts, then the order is still there.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_new_order_is_still_there_after_a_restart`.

### AC-08

Given the Study page, when the learner drags a card, or ticked cards together, or presses the up or down arrow on a card, then the cards move and the new order is saved.

Checked by hand in the browser, because the frontend has no automated test yet.
