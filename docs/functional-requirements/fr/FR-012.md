# FR-012: Make Decks And Keep Each Card In Decks Of Its Language Pair

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-11`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/201), [`DEC-020`](../../decisions.md#dec-020), [`DEC-047`](../../decisions.md#dec-047), [`DEC-064`](../../decisions.md#dec-064)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - The product gives each marked word of a text with its card, or with no card when the card was deleted ([`DEC-064`](../../decisions.md#dec-064)).

## Behavior

When the learner makes a deck, the product shall ask for a title and a pair of languages, the language of the texts and the learner's first language.

The product shall refuse a pair whose two languages are the same.

The product shall remove the extra spaces of the title.

The product shall refuse a title that has no characters or more than 60.

The product shall refuse a title that another deck of the same pair of languages already has, whatever the case of the letters.

The product shall accept a title that a deck of another pair of languages has.

The product shall make a new deck with no cards.

The product shall list the decks, the newest first, each with its number of cards.

When the learner asks for the decks of one pair of languages, the product shall list only the decks of that pair.

The product shall keep for each card its own pair of languages, and shall not tie the card to a text.

The product shall let a card be in several decks.

When a card is put in a deck of another pair of languages than its own, the product shall refuse it and save nothing.

The product shall keep the same word and phrase in two pairs of languages as two cards.

When the text of a card is deleted, the product shall keep the card and the decks it is in.

The product shall give each marked word of a text with the card made for it, and with no card when the word has none or its card was deleted.

When the product starts with cards that were made before the decks, it shall put them in one deck titled "Earlier cards" for each pair of languages that has cards.

When the product puts the cards made before the decks in a deck, it shall keep the languages, the sentences and the study data of every card, the order of the study queue, and the words of each text with their cards on the text page.

The product shall make no deck for a pair of languages without cards.

The product shall move the cards made before the decks into decks once, and when it fails, it shall change nothing.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a title with extra spaces, when the learner makes a deck, then the deck has the clean title and no cards.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_deck_is_made_with_a_clean_title_and_starts_empty`.

### AC-02

Given a title of no characters, only spaces or 61 characters, when the learner makes a deck, then the product refuses it and no deck is made.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_deck_title_must_have_1_to_60_characters`.

### AC-03

Given the same language for the texts and for the learner, when the learner makes a deck, then the product refuses it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_deck_needs_two_different_languages`.

### AC-04

Given a deck "Travel", when the learner makes "tRAVEL" for the same pair, then the product refuses it, and when the learner makes "Travel" for another pair, then it is made.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_title_is_taken_for_the_language_pair_whatever_the_case_of_the_letters`.

### AC-05

Given decks of two pairs of languages, when the learner lists the decks, then they come newest first, and with a pair only the decks of that pair come.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_decks_are_listed_with_their_cards_and_can_be_kept_to_a_language_pair`.

### AC-06

Given a card and a deck of another pair of languages, when the card is put in the deck, then the product refuses it and saves nothing.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_deck_only_takes_cards_of_its_language_pair_and_nothing_is_saved_otherwise`.

### AC-07

Given the same word and phrase in two pairs of languages, when the cards are made, then they are two cards.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_same_word_and_phrase_can_be_a_card_in_two_language_pairs`.

### AC-08

Given a card in two decks, when the decks are listed, then the card is counted once in each deck.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_card_can_be_in_several_decks_and_a_deck_is_listed_once_per_card`.

### AC-09

Given a card of a text, when the text is deleted, then the card stays.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_card_stays_when_its_text_is_deleted`.

### AC-10

Given a database with cards made before the decks, when the product starts, then every card is in "Earlier cards" of its pair of languages and keeps its languages and study data.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_migration_keeps_every_card_with_its_languages_and_its_study_data` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_migration_puts_the_cards_that_exist_in_one_deck_for_each_language_pair`.

### AC-11

Given a database without cards, or without cards in one pair of languages, when the product starts, then no deck is made for it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_database_without_cards_gets_no_deck` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_migration_makes_no_deck_for_a_language_pair_without_cards`.

### AC-12

Given cards made before the decks, when the product starts, then the text page shows the words of each text with their cards and the study queue keeps its order.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_migration_keeps_the_cards_of_each_text_on_the_text_page` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_migration_keeps_the_order_of_the_study_queue`.

### AC-13

Given a database that was migrated, when the product starts again, then the migration does not run a second time, and when a migration fails, then nothing changes.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_migration_runs_only_once` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_migration_that_fails_changes_nothing`.

### AC-14

Given a text with a marked word that has a card and one that has none, when the learner opens the text or lists the texts, then each marked word comes with its card or with no card, and a word whose card was deleted comes with no card.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_an_opened_text_returns_each_marked_word_with_its_card_or_with_no_card`, `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_the_list_shows_each_text_with_its_marked_words_and_cards` and `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_word_whose_card_was_deleted_is_a_mark_with_no_card`.
