# FR-006: Hide A Card From Study

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`DEC-016`](../../decisions.md#dec-016)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner hides a card, the product shall keep the card in its place in the cards table and shall leave it out of the study queue and the study session.

When the learner shows a hidden card again, the product shall bring it back to the study queue and the study session.

The product shall hide or show several chosen cards in one action.

When the learner edits the sentence of a hidden card, the product shall keep the card hidden.

When one of the chosen cards does not exist, the product shall hide or show none of the chosen cards.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a hidden card, when the cards table and the study session are read, then the card is in its place in the table and not in the session.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_hidden_card_stays_in_its_place_in_the_cards_table_but_is_not_studied`.

### AC-02

Given a hidden card, when the learner shows it again, then it is in the study session.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_hidden_card_that_is_shown_again_comes_back_to_the_study_session`.

### AC-03

Given several cards, when the learner hides them in one action and shows them in one action, then they leave and come back together.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_several_cards_are_hidden_in_one_action_and_shown_again_in_one_action`.

### AC-04

Given a hidden card, when its sentence is edited, then it stays hidden.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_editing_the_sentence_of_a_hidden_card_keeps_it_hidden`.

### AC-05

Given a hidden card, when the study queue is read, then the card is not in it and the other cards can still be ordered.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_hidden_card_is_not_in_the_queue_and_the_rest_can_still_be_ordered`.

### AC-06

Given chosen cards of which one does not exist, when they are hidden, then none of them is hidden.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_when_one_card_to_hide_does_not_exist_none_is_hidden`.
