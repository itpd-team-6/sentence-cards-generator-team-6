# FR-022: Manage The Decks And Open The Cards Of A Deck

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-20`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/253), [`DEC-065`](../../decisions.md#dec-065), [`DEC-069`](../../decisions.md#dec-069)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`, `database`

## Behavior

The product shall have a page "Decks", the fourth tab, with a tile for each deck that shows its title, the number of cards to study today, the share of its cards that are learned and its number of cards.
The tiles shall be in a section for each language pair that has a deck, with the name of the pair and its number of decks.
The deck "Uncategorized" shall be a tile like the others, after the other decks of its pair.
When there is more than one language pair, the page shall have a button for each pair that shows only the chosen pairs, and from six decks a search by title; with nothing chosen all the decks show.
A tile shall open the page of that deck, at an address of its own.

The learner shall make a deck on the page "Decks" with a title and a language pair chosen with the same buttons as the form that adds a text, with the rules of [`FR-012`](FR-012.md), and shall rename a deck in place, on its page, with the same rules for the title.
The product shall not rename the deck "Uncategorized".

The page of a deck shall show its title, its language pair, its number of cards, the share learned, the number of new, forgotten and review cards due today in it and about how long they take, and, when cards of the deck are due today, a button that opens the Study page with only this deck ticked and starts the session at once, and the arrow of that session shall go back to the page of the deck.
It shall list the cards of the deck, which the learner searches, edits, regenerates, hides and deletes as in "Your cards" ([`FR-020`](FR-020.md)), and shall let the learner take cards out of the deck and add cards of the same language pair to it.
The product shall not offer the deck "Uncategorized" to add cards to by hand.

The opened card of a list of cards shall show its decks as links, and a link shall open that deck.

When the learner deletes a deck, the product shall say how many of its cards are only in that deck and how many are also in another deck.
The product shall never delete a card that is also in another deck.
For the cards that are only in the deleted deck, the learner shall choose to delete them or to keep them in the deck "Uncategorized" of their language pair, and the product shall make that deck if it does not exist.
The product shall not delete a deck that has cards only in it until the learner has chosen, and when something fails it shall change nothing.
The deck "Uncategorized" shall be deleted only together with its cards.

The product shall keep every card in at least one deck: a card taken out of its last deck shall go to the deck "Uncategorized" of its language pair, and a card put in another deck shall leave it.
A migration shall add the mark of the deck "Uncategorized" to the decks, shall put every card that is in no deck in the deck "Uncategorized" of its language pair, and shall take a deck that the learner titled "Uncategorized" as that deck.

The product shall show the estimated time of today's cards in the panel of the header, not in the pill ([`DEC-069`](../../decisions.md#dec-069), [`FR-021`](FR-021.md)).

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given decks, when the learner opens the tab "Decks", then each deck is a tile with its cards to study today, its share learned and its number of cards, and the deck "Uncategorized" is the last of its language pair, and the tiles are in a section for each pair; the learner makes a deck with a title and a language pair, and renames a deck, and the deck "Uncategorized" cannot be renamed.

Test: `frontend/src/DecksPage.test.jsx::each deck is a tile that opens the deck, with its cards to study today, its share learned and its cards`, `frontend/src/DecksPage.test.jsx::the deck Uncategorized is a tile like the others, shown after them`, `frontend/src/DecksPage.test.jsx::the decks are in a section for each language pair that has a deck, with the number of decks`, `frontend/src/DecksPage.test.jsx::the language pairs are buttons that show only the chosen ones, and none chosen shows all`, `frontend/src/DecksPage.test.jsx::from six decks a search by title shows up, and a search with no result can be cleared`, `frontend/src/DecksPage.test.jsx::New deck makes a deck with a title and a language pair chosen with the language buttons, and it shows in the list`, `frontend/src/DeckPage.test.jsx::Rename turns the title into a field in place, Save changes it, and a title the product refuses is shown`, `frontend/src/DeckPage.test.jsx::Cancel leaves the title as it was`, `frontend/src/DeckPage.test.jsx::the deck Uncategorized cannot be renamed and cards are not added to it by hand`, `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_deck_is_renamed_with_the_rules_of_a_new_title`, `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_title_that_another_deck_of_the_pair_has_is_refused` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_deck_uncategorized_cannot_be_renamed`.

### AC-02

### AC-03
