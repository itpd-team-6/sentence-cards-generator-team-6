# FR-018: Study In A Session That Shows Only The Card, And Fix The Card There

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/29), [`US-02`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/28), [`DEC-063`](../../decisions.md#dec-063), [`DEC-064`](../../decisions.md#dec-064)
- **Containers:** `frontend`
- **Changed:**
  - The list of all the cards moved from the page that adds a text to the page "Your cards" ([`DEC-064`](../../decisions.md#dec-064)).

## Behavior

When the learner starts a session, the product shall hide the header and the tabs, so that the page shows only the card.

The session shall have an arrow at the top left that ends the session and goes back to the Study page, where the header and the tabs are shown again.

Before the learner asks for it, the session shall show the card without the translation and without any text in its place.

The session shall let the learner, without leaving it, edit the sentence and the translation of the card in front of them, as [`FR-003`](FR-003.md) says, ask for a new sentence for it, and hide it, as [`FR-006`](FR-006.md) says.

When the learner asks for a new sentence, the session shall put the new sentence and its translation on the same card, hide the translation again and go on with that card.

When the learner hides the card, the session shall go on with the next card, and the hidden card shall not come back in the session.

When the learner opens the Your cards page after changing a card in a session, the list of cards there shall show the card as it is now.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session, when it starts, then the header and the tabs are hidden, and when the learner uses the arrow at the top left, then the session ends and the Study page is shown with them again.

Test: `frontend/src/App.test.jsx::the header and the tabs step aside while a study session is open`, `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the page around is told when a session is open and when it ends` and `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the arrow at the top left leaves the session and goes back to Study`.

### AC-02

Given a card in a session, when it is shown, then it has no text in place of the translation, and the translation appears only when the learner asks for it.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::a session shows no hint text before the translation, and the translation only on request`.

### AC-03

Given a card in a session, when the learner edits its sentence and saves, then the card shows the new sentence and the session goes on with it.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::a card can be edited inside the session, which goes on with it`.

### AC-04

Given a card in a session with its translation shown, when the learner asks for a new sentence, then the same card shows the new sentence with the translation hidden.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::a new sentence replaces the old one on the same card, and the translation is hidden again`.

### AC-05

Given a card in a session, when the learner hides it, then the session goes on with the next card and the hidden card does not come back.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::a hidden card leaves the session and does not come back`.

### AC-06

Given a card hidden in a session, when the learner opens the Your cards page, then the list of cards shows it as hidden.

Test: `frontend/src/App.test.jsx::the Your cards page is made when the learner goes to it`, and checked by hand in the browser for the list itself.
