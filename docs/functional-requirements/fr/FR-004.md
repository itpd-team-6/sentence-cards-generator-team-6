# FR-004: Delete Cards

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/28), [`DEC-005`](../../decisions.md#dec-005), [`DEC-016`](../../decisions.md#dec-016)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner deletes one card, the product shall delete that card only.

When the learner asks to delete several chosen cards, the product shall ask for confirmation, and shall delete the chosen cards only after the learner confirms.

When the learner cancels the confirmation, the product shall delete no card.

When one of the chosen cards does not exist, the product shall delete none of the chosen cards.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given several cards, when the learner deletes one, then only that card is gone.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_deleting_a_card_removes_only_that_card`.

### AC-02

Given several cards, when the learner chooses some and confirms, then those cards are deleted in one action and the others stay.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_chosen_cards_are_deleted_in_one_action_and_the_others_stay`.

### AC-03

Given chosen cards of which one does not exist, when they are deleted, then none of them is deleted.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_when_one_card_to_delete_does_not_exist_none_is_deleted`.

### AC-04

Given three ticked cards, when the learner clicks the button to delete them, then a window asks for confirmation, no card is deleted until the learner confirms, and cancelling deletes none.

Checked by hand in the browser, because the frontend has no automated test yet.
