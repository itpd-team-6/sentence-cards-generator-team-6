# FR-019: Keep The Texts Of The Learner And Edit Them With Their Cards

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-20`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/253), [`DEC-058`](../../decisions.md#dec-058), [`DEC-064`](../../decisions.md#dec-064)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`, `database`

## Behavior

The product shall keep each text the learner saves, with its title, its pair of languages and its marked words, until the learner deletes it.

When the learner gives no title, the product shall make one from the first words of the text, six words at most and 60 characters at most.

The product shall remove the extra spaces of a title, and shall refuse a title that has no characters or more than 60.

When the product starts with texts that were saved before the titles, it shall give each one the title it would have made.

The product shall refuse a text of more than 10,000 characters, and the message shall say the maximum.

The page that adds a text shall show next to the text how many characters it has of the 10,000, in red when it has reached the maximum, and shall not send a text that is over it.

The Texts page shall list the texts, the newest first, each with its title, its pair of languages, the number of its marked words and of its cards, and its marked words, the first four and how many more there are.

The learner shall be able to open a text from the list on a page of its own, with the address `#/texts/` and the number of the text.

The page of a text shall show the title, the text, the marked words with the ones that have no card drawn differently, the decks to put the new cards in, and the cards of this text.

The learner shall be able to change the title, the text and the marked words of a text, and the product shall keep the changes only when the learner chooses "Save changes".

The product shall not let the learner change the pair of languages of a saved text.

When the learner takes a mark away, the product shall remove only the mark: the card stays in "Your cards" and in its decks, with its study data, and it is no longer one of the cards of this text.

When the learner changes the text, the product shall keep the mark of a word that the new text still has, with its card, even when the case of the letters changed.

The product shall keep a mark of several words while the new text has its words as whole words in this order, and shall show the learner a mark of several words at the first place where its words are found in this order.

When the new text no longer has the words of a mark, the product shall remove the mark, shall say which marks it removed, and shall keep their cards.

The product shall not mark a word that the text does not have.

When the learner asks for the cards of a text with changes that are not saved, the product shall save the changes first and then make the cards of the words that have none, as [`FR-001`](FR-001.md) says.

The learner shall be able to delete a text after confirming it, and the product shall say that its cards, their decks and the study data stay, and shall remove only the text and its marks, as [`FR-012`](FR-012.md) says.

The product shall tell each card the texts that still have its word, with the title of each, and the box that edits a card shall list them with a link to the page of each, and shall list none for a card that no text has.

The product shall show all the cards of the learner on a page of its own, "Your cards", with the address `#/your-cards`, and the page of a text shall show only the cards of that text.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a text with no title, when the learner saves it, then its title is its first words, six at most and 60 characters at most, and a title the learner gives is kept without the extra spaces.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_text_gets_a_title_made_from_its_first_words` and `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_saved_text_has_a_title_and_the_title_can_be_given`.

### AC-02

Given a title of only spaces or of 61 characters, when the learner changes the title, then the product refuses it, and a title of 1 to 60 characters is kept.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_the_title_needs_one_to_sixty_characters`.

### AC-03

Given a database with texts saved before the titles, when the product starts, then each text has the title made from its first words.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_database_with_earlier_texts_gives_each_one_a_title`.

### AC-04

Given a text of more than 10,000 characters, when the learner saves it, then the product refuses it with a message that gives the maximum and saves nothing, and a text of exactly 10,000 characters is saved.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_text_longer_than_the_maximum_is_refused_and_the_message_says_the_maximum` and `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_text_of_exactly_the_maximum_is_saved`.

### AC-05

Given a text over the maximum on the page that adds a text, when the learner asks for the sentences, then nothing is sent and the message gives the maximum, and the count is red from the maximum.

Test: `frontend/src/TextPage.test.jsx::a text over the maximum is not sent, and the message gives the maximum` and `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::the count of characters is next to the text, and it is red when the text reaches the maximum`.

### AC-06

Given a text over the maximum on the page of a saved text, when the learner saves it, then nothing is sent and the message gives the maximum.

Test: `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::a text over the maximum is not saved, and the message gives the maximum`.

### AC-07

Given saved texts, when the learner opens the Texts page, then each text is listed with its title, languages, marked words and cards, the newest first, with a link to its page, and the marked words without a card are drawn with a dashed ring.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_the_list_shows_each_text_with_its_marked_words_and_cards`, `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_the_list_has_the_newest_text_first_and_counts_cards_once`, `frontend/src/TextList.test.jsx::each text shows its title, languages, marked words and cards, and links to its page` and `frontend/src/TextList.test.jsx::the chips show the marked words, the ones without a card with a dashed ring, and say how many more there are`.

### AC-08

Given a saved text, when the learner opens it, then the page shows its title, its languages, its marked words and its cards, and a marked word without a card says so.

Test: `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::the saved text opens with its title, its languages, its marked words and its cards` and `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::a marked word without a card has a dashed chip that says so`.

### AC-09

Given a new title, when the learner saves, then only the title is sent, and nothing is saved before the learner chooses "Save changes".

Test: `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::a new title is saved alone` and `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::taking a mark away changes nothing until the learner saves, and then only the mark goes`.

### AC-10

Given a saved text, when the learner changes the title and the text, then the cards stay as they were.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_editing_the_title_and_the_text_keeps_the_cards_as_they_were`.

### AC-11

Given a change with the pair of languages, when it is sent, then the product refuses it, and an unknown text cannot be changed or deleted.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_change_with_an_unknown_field_is_refused` and `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_an_unknown_text_cannot_be_changed_or_deleted`.

### AC-12

Given a mark whose word the new text no longer has, when the learner saves the text, then the mark is removed, the answer names it, and the card stays.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_mark_whose_word_is_no_longer_in_the_text_is_removed_and_told`, `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_change_names_the_marks_the_new_text_took_away` and `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::a mark whose word the edited text no longer has waits as lost and goes when the text is saved`.

### AC-13

Given a mark that the new text still has, when the case of its letters changed, then the mark keeps its card, and a mark of several words stays while its words are in the text in this order.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_mark_that_stays_keeps_its_card_when_the_case_of_the_letters_changes`, `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_mark_of_several_words_stays_while_its_words_are_in_the_text_in_order`, `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_mark_is_in_a_text_when_its_words_are_whole_words_in_order` and `frontend/src/words.test.js::a saved group of words is found in its order, with other words allowed between`.

### AC-14

Given a word that the text does not have, when the learner marks it, then the product refuses it, and the marks the learner keeps are the marks the text has.

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_word_that_is_not_in_the_text_cannot_be_marked` and `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_the_words_the_learner_keeps_are_the_marks_the_text_has`.

### AC-15

Given a marked word with a card, when the learner takes its mark away and saves, then the card is no longer one of the cards of the text and stays in "Your cards".

Test: `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::a card whose mark was taken away leaves the cards of this text when the learner saves`, and checked by hand in the browser for the card in "Your cards".

### AC-16

Given a text with unsaved changes, when the learner asks for the cards, then the changes are saved first and the cards are made in the ticked decks.

Test: `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::making cards saves the changes first and then makes the cards in the ticked decks`.

### AC-17

Given a text with cards, when the learner deletes it, then the product asks for confirmation, says that the cards stay, and keeps the cards, their decks and their study data.

Test: `frontend/src/SavedText.test.jsx::a text is deleted only after the learner confirms, and its cards are said to stay` and `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_deleting_a_text_keeps_its_cards_their_decks_and_their_study_data`.

### AC-18

Test: `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_a_card_shows_each_text_that_still_has_its_word_and_none_when_no_text_has_it`, `backend/tests/test_text_library.py::test_the_cards_of_the_api_carry_the_texts_they_come_from`, `frontend/src/CardEditDialog.test.jsx::the box lists the texts the card comes from, each with a link to its page` and `frontend/src/CardEditDialog.test.jsx::following a link closes the box`.

### AC-20
