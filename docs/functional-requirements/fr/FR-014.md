# FR-014: Study Only The Decks The Learner Ticks

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-11`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/201), [`DEC-047`](../../decisions.md#dec-047), [`DEC-048`](../../decisions.md#dec-048)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

The Study page shall list the decks above the cards, each with a box to tick it and the number of cards due today in it.

The Study page shall give a button that ticks every deck, and that clears them when every deck is ticked.

The first time the learner opens the Study page, no deck shall be ticked.

The learner shall not be able to start a session until a deck is ticked.

The product shall remember the ticked decks in the browser.

The product shall not tick a remembered deck that no longer exists.

When decks are ticked, the study session and the study queue shall have only the cards that are in at least one of the ticked decks.

A card that is in two ticked decks shall be in the session and in the queue once.

Each card of the queue shall show all the decks it is in.

When the learner saves a new order of the queue, the product shall save the order of the cards of the ticked decks only, and shall leave the cards of the other decks as they were.

When the cards of the ticked decks are not the ones the learner saw, the product shall refuse the order, as [`FR-008`](FR-008.md) says.

The learner shall be able to shuffle the cards of one session, and the Study box shall say that the session is shuffled.

The Study page shall have two boxes side by side, a Study box to choose the decks and start a session, and a Reorder box to move the cards.

The Reorder box shall start folded, and the learner shall be able to open it and fold it again.

The Reorder box shall list the cards due today and the cards due later in two scroll boxes, and the learner shall be able to move a card from one to the other.

Until the decks are loaded, the Study page shall show only a short note, so that the page does not jump when they appear.

When the learner ticks or unticks a deck, the Study page shall keep the height of each of its boxes, so that the page does not move.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given cards in two decks and no ticked deck, when the product gives the queue and the due cards, then it gives every card.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_without_ticked_decks_the_queue_and_the_due_cards_have_every_card`.

### AC-02

Given two decks, when the learner ticks one, then the queue and the due cards have only the cards of that deck.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_only_the_cards_of_the_ticked_decks_are_studied`.

### AC-03

Given a card in two ticked decks, when the queue is read, then the card is there once, and it shows both of its decks.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_card_in_two_ticked_decks_is_in_the_queue_once` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_card_in_a_ticked_deck_shows_all_its_decks`.

### AC-04

Given a new order saved for some decks, when the queue of every deck is read, then the order is the one the learner saved.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_order_saved_for_some_decks_is_the_order_of_every_deck` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_reorder_in_the_database_uses_the_cards_of_the_given_decks_only`.

### AC-05

Given cards that changed in the ticked decks, when the learner saves an order, then the product refuses it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_an_order_is_refused_when_the_cards_of_the_ticked_decks_changed`.

### AC-06

Given a card in an unticked deck, when the learner saves an order of the ticked decks, then the card of the unticked deck keeps its place.

Test: `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_a_card_in_an_unticked_deck_does_not_change_the_order_of_the_ticked_ones`.

### AC-07

Given the Study page the first time, when it opens, then no deck is ticked and the session cannot start.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::no deck is ticked the first time, so a session cannot start`.

### AC-08

Given ticked decks, when the Study box is read, then it counts the cards due today in each ticked deck, and a session has only the cards of the ticked decks.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the Study box counts the cards due today in each ticked deck` and `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::a session has only the cards of the ticked decks`.

### AC-09

Given the decks, when the learner presses the button that ticks every deck, then all are ticked, and when all are ticked, it clears them.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the button ticks every deck, and clears them when all are ticked`.

### AC-10

Given ticked decks, when the learner opens the page again in the same browser, then they are ticked, and a deck that no longer exists is not.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the ticked decks are remembered in this browser`, `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::a remembered deck that no longer exists is not ticked` and `frontend/src/deckSelection.test.js`.

### AC-11

Given a session, when the learner shuffles it, then it has the same cards in another order and the box says so.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the session can be shuffled, and the box says so` and `frontend/src/deckSelection.test.js::shuffling gives the same cards in another order and leaves the list alone`.

### AC-12

Given the Study page, when it opens, then the Reorder box is folded, it opens and folds again, and until the decks are loaded only a short note is drawn.

Test: `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::the Reorder box starts folded away, and it can be opened and folded again` and `frontend/src/StudyPage.test.jsx::only a short note is drawn until the decks are loaded`.

### AC-13

Given the Reorder box with the cards due today and the cards due later, when the learner looks at it, then each part has its own scroll box and cards can be dragged from one to the other.

Checked by hand in the browser, because the drag of cards has no automated test, as [`FR-008`](FR-008.md) AC-08 says.

### AC-14

Given the Study page scrolled to the bottom, when the learner ticks and unticks decks with the Reorder box folded and with it open, then the page does not move, on a wide window and on a phone.

Checked by hand in the browser, because a test in a simulated page cannot measure the height of a box.
