# FR-021: Show The Cards To Study Today In The Header

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-17`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/234), [`DEC-066`](../../decisions.md#dec-066), [`DEC-068`](../../decisions.md#dec-068)
- **Containers:** `frontend`

## Behavior

The product shall show, in the header of every page except the Study page, a pill that says how many cards the learner has to study today and about how long they take, written as "8 to study" and "about 4 min", with the estimate of [`DEC-066`](../../decisions.md#dec-066).
The pill shall count the cards due today or earlier of all the decks, not only the decks ticked on the Study page, and a card that is in two decks shall count once.
A click on the pill shall open the Study page.

While the pointer rests on the pill, or the pill has the keyboard focus, the product shall show a panel under it, "Today — All decks", with the number of new, forgotten and review cards, and the number of cards due today in each deck, the decks with the most cards first.
A card that is in two decks shall count in each of them and once in the total.
The panel shall not open on a screen that has no pointer, and a screen reader shall read it as the description of the pill.

When no card is due today, the pill shall say "All done today" and the day of the next card, such as "next Sun 15 Nov", and when the learner has no card with a sentence, it shall say "No cards yet".
When the cards cannot be read, the header shall show no pill.

The header shall be as wide as the window, with its content lined up with the page, shall not stay fixed when the page scrolls, and shall be hidden while a study session is open, as [`FR-018`](FR-018.md) says.
The pill shall read the cards again when the learner opens another page and when a change of the learner's data succeeds, so that the number is not out of date.
Where the bar has no room, the product shall drop first the time and then the name of the product.

The product shall read the cards with the calls that the Study page already uses, and shall add no endpoint and no migration.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given cards due today, when the learner is on any page but Study, then the header has a pill with the number of cards to study and the estimated time, and a click on it opens the Study page.

Test: `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::the pill says how many cards are to study and about how long, and opens the Study page` and `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::it asks for the cards of all the decks, not of some`.

### AC-02

Given cards due today in two decks, one of them in both, when the pointer rests on the pill or it has the focus, then the panel gives the cards of each kind and of each deck, and the card that is in both decks is counted once in the total.

Test: `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::the panel gives the cards of each kind and of each deck, a card in two decks once in the total`, `frontend/src/todaySummary.test.js::a card in two decks counts once in the total and once in each deck` and `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::the pill is described by the panel, so a screen reader reads it too`.

### AC-03

Given no card due today, when the learner is on any page but Study, then the pill says all is done and the day of the next card, and when there is no card at all it says there are no cards yet.

Test: `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::with nothing due, it says all is done and when the next card is due` and `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::with no card at all, it says there are no cards yet`.

### AC-04

Given the Study page or a failed read, then the header has no pill, and the Study page does not ask for the cards for it.

Test: `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::it shows nothing on the Study page, and does not ask for the cards there`, `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::it shows nothing when the cards cannot be read` and `frontend/src/App.test.jsx::the summary of today is in the header on every page but Study`.

### AC-05

Given a change of the learner's data, when the call succeeds, then the pill reads the cards again.

Test: `frontend/src/TodaySummary.test.jsx::it reads the cards again when a call changed something`.

### AC-06

Given a study session, then the header is hidden.

Test: `frontend/src/App.test.jsx::the header and the tabs step aside while a study session is open`.
