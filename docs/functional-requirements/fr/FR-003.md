# FR-003: Edit The Sentence And The Translation Of A Card

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/28), [`DEC-028`](../../decisions.md#dec-028)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner saves an edited sentence for a card, the product shall replace the sentence of that card only and shall keep it after a restart.

When the sentence of a card is edited and its translation is not locked, the product shall make a new translation of the new sentence, unless the learner saves a translation in the same request.

When the learner saves a translation, the product shall keep it as written.

When the learner locks the translation of a card, the product shall not change that translation when the sentence is edited, and shall keep the lock until the learner unlocks it.

The product shall refuse to lock a card that has no translation.

When the learner saves a sentence that has not changed, the product shall not ask the language model again.

When the new translation cannot be made, the product shall change nothing on the card and shall report the reason.

The product shall refuse an empty sentence.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given several cards, when the learner edits the sentence of one and saves it, then only that card changes and it keeps the edit after a restart.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_editing_a_sentence_changes_only_that_card_and_it_survives_a_restart`.

### AC-02

Given a card whose translation is not locked, when its sentence is edited, then the translation is made again from the new sentence.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_new_translation_is_made_for_an_edited_sentence`.

### AC-03

Given a card, when the learner saves the same sentence, then the model is not asked.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_an_unchanged_sentence_does_not_ask_the_model_again`.

### AC-04

Given a model that cannot translate, when the learner saves an edited sentence, then the card keeps its sentence and its translation and the reason is reported.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_when_the_translation_fails_the_card_keeps_its_sentence_and_its_translation`.

### AC-05

Given a card, when the learner saves an empty sentence, then the product refuses and the card does not change.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_an_empty_edit_or_an_empty_sentence_is_refused`.

### AC-06

Given a card, when the learner saves a translation, then the card keeps it as written, and when the learner saves a new sentence and a translation together, then the translation written is the one kept.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_learner_can_write_the_translation_and_it_is_saved_as_written` and `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_translation_written_in_the_same_save_as_a_new_sentence_is_the_one_kept`.

### AC-07

Given a card with a translation, when the learner locks it, then the card says it is locked until the learner unlocks it, and a card without a translation cannot be locked.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_learner_locks_and_unlocks_the_translation_of_a_card_with_one_click` and `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_card_without_a_translation_cannot_be_locked`.

### AC-08

Given a card whose translation is locked, when the sentence is edited, then the translation does not change, and the learner can still edit the translation.

Test: `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_a_locked_translation_stays_when_the_sentence_is_edited` and `backend/tests/test_cards.py::test_the_learner_can_edit_a_locked_translation_and_it_stays_locked`.
