# FR-007: Offer The Due Cards And Set When A Card Comes Back

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/29), [`US-10`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/150), [`DEC-018`](../../decisions.md#dec-018), [`DEC-019`](../../decisions.md#dec-019), [`DEC-029`](../../decisions.md#dec-029)
- **Containers:** `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - The learner can set how many new cards and how many review cards they want each day, and can ask for a number of cards when too few are due ([`DEC-019`](../../decisions.md#dec-019)).

## Behavior

When the learner starts a study session, the product shall offer only the cards that are due today or earlier and have a sentence.

When the product offers a card, it shall also say, for each of the four answers "again", "hard", "good" and "easy", how many days the card would wait and the day it would come back, so that the buttons can show it.

When the product offers a card, it shall also say its stage: "new" when the learner never answered it, "forgotten" when it was answered and has no wait, as after "again", and "due" when it was answered and its wait has passed.

The study page shall call the stage "due" Review, so that the word does not mix with the cards that are due today, which are all three stages.

When the learner answers "again", the product shall keep the card due today, so that it comes back in the same session, and shall start its wait over.

When the learner answers "hard", "good" or "easy", the product shall take the card out of the session and shall set its next due date by the rule of [`DEC-029`](../../decisions.md#dec-029): each of the three answers is at least one day longer than the wait of the card, "good" longer than "hard" and "easy" longer than "good", and none is longer than 365 days.

The product shall keep each answer after a restart.

The product shall keep, for the library, a limit of new cards a day and a limit of review cards a day, each one a whole number from 0 to 1000 or not set, and shall keep them after a restart.

The limits shall never keep out of a session a card that is due today or earlier, and setting them shall not change any due date.

When the learner asks for a number of cards, the product shall offer every card that is due today or earlier and, when they are fewer than the number, the cards due next after them until there are as many as asked, each card once and only from the ticked decks, as [`FR-014`](FR-014.md) says.

Asking for a number of cards shall not change any due date: a card offered ahead of its day gets its due date only when the learner answers it, by the rule above.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given cards due today, earlier and later, when a session starts, then only the cards due today or earlier are in it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_only_the_cards_due_today_or_earlier_are_in_the_session`.

### AC-02

Given a card without a sentence, when a session starts, then the card is not in it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_without_a_sentence_is_not_studied`.

### AC-03

Given a due card, when the learner answers "good", then it leaves the session and is due later than today.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_good_answer_takes_the_card_out_of_the_session_and_makes_it_due_later_than_today`.

### AC-04

Given a card with a wait of 8 days, when the learner answers "hard", "good" or "easy", then the card waits 10, 16 or 21 days, and each wait is longer than the one before it and none passes 365 days.

Test: `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_the_waits_of_a_card_that_has_one_follow_the_rule_of_the_decision`, `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_each_answer_but_again_waits_longer_than_the_one_before_it_and_than_the_card_did` and `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_the_wait_never_goes_past_the_longest_interval`.

### AC-05

Given a due card, when the learner answers "again", then it is still due today and comes back in the session.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_an_again_answer_leaves_the_card_due_today_so_it_comes_back_in_the_session`.

### AC-06

Given answers that were given, when the product restarts, then they are still there.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_answers_are_still_there_after_a_restart`.

### AC-07

Given a due card, when the session offers it, then it says for each of the four answers how many days the card would wait and the day it would come back, and the answer then gives the card that day.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_each_card_of_the_session_says_when_it_comes_back_for_each_of_the_four_answers` and `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_each_answer_gives_the_card_the_due_date_that_its_button_said`.

### AC-08

Given a card that was never answered, when the learner answers it "again", then the session says it is new before the answer and forgotten after it, and a card with a wait is due.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_is_new_until_it_is_answered_forgotten_after_again_and_due_after_a_good_answer` and `backend/tests/test_decks.py::test_the_answer_count_starts_at_one_for_a_card_that_already_waits`.

### AC-09

Given limits of 1 new card and 2 review cards, when the learner saves them, then the library shows both values and they are still there after a restart.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_limits_start_as_not_set`, `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_limits_are_saved_separately_and_read_again` and `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_limit_below_zero_is_refused`.

### AC-10

Given 3 new cards and 4 review cards due today and limits of 1 and 2, when a session starts, then all 7 cards are in it and no due date changed.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_limits_keep_no_due_card_out_of_the_session_and_change_no_due_date`.

### AC-11

Given no card due today and cards due tomorrow and the day after, when the learner asks for cards, then the card due first comes first, and the due dates stay as they were until a card is answered by the rule of the scheduler.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_due_later_is_offered_ahead_of_its_day_when_the_learner_asks_for_cards`, `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_asking_for_one_card_ahead_gives_the_card_due_next` and `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_that_is_answered_ahead_of_its_day_gets_the_due_date_of_the_scheduler`.

### AC-12

Given 5 cards in the ticked decks, 3 due today or earlier and 2 due later, with one card in two ticked decks, when the learner asks for 4 cards, then the session has the 3 due cards and the earliest later card, and when the learner asks for 10, then it has the 5 cards, each once, and none from an unticked deck.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_target_fills_the_session_with_the_earliest_later_cards_of_the_ticked_decks_once`, `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_target_lower_than_the_cards_due_today_does_not_withhold_any_of_them` and `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_target_below_one_is_refused`.
