# FR-007: Offer The Due Cards And Set When A Card Comes Back

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/29)
- **Containers:** `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner starts a study session, the product shall offer only the cards that are due today or earlier and have a sentence.

When the learner answers that a card was remembered, the product shall take the card out of the session and shall set its next due date later than today, each remembered answer putting the card further away than the last, up to 365 days.

When the learner answers that a card was forgotten, the product shall keep the card due today, so that it comes back in the same session, and shall start its wait over.

The product shall keep each answer after a restart.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given cards due today, earlier and later, when a session starts, then only the cards due today or earlier are in it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_only_the_cards_due_today_or_earlier_are_in_the_session`.

### AC-02

Given a card without a sentence, when a session starts, then the card is not in it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_without_a_sentence_is_not_studied`.

### AC-03

Given a due card, when the learner answers that it was remembered, then it leaves the session and is due later than today.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_remembered_card_leaves_the_session_and_is_due_later_than_today`.

### AC-04

Given a card remembered again and again, when each answer is given, then each wait is longer than the last and none passes 365 days.

Test: `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_each_remembered_answer_puts_the_card_further_away_than_the_last` and `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_the_wait_never_goes_past_the_longest_interval`.

### AC-05

Given a due card, when the learner answers that it was forgotten, then it is still due today and comes back in the session.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_forgotten_card_is_still_due_today_so_it_comes_back_in_the_session`.

### AC-06

Given answers that were given, when the product restarts, then they are still there.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_answers_are_still_there_after_a_restart`.
