# FR-007: Offer The Due Cards And Set When A Card Comes Back

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/29), [`DEC-018`](../../decisions.md#dec-018), [`DEC-029`](../../decisions.md#dec-029)
- **Containers:** `backend`

## Behavior

When the learner starts a study session, the product shall offer only the cards that are due today or earlier and have a sentence.

When the product offers a card, it shall also say, for each of the four answers "again", "hard", "good" and "easy", how many days the card would wait and the day it would come back, so that the buttons can show it.

When the learner answers "again", the product shall keep the card due today, so that it comes back in the same session, and shall start its wait over.

When the learner answers "hard", "good" or "easy", the product shall take the card out of the session and shall set its next due date by the rule of [`DEC-029`](../../decisions.md#dec-029): each of the three answers is at least one day longer than the wait of the card, "good" longer than "hard" and "easy" longer than "good", and none is longer than 365 days.

The product shall keep each answer after a restart.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given cards due today, earlier and later, when a session starts, then only the cards due today or earlier are in it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_only_the_cards_due_today_or_earlier_are_in_the_session`.

### AC-02

Given a card without a sentence, when a session starts, then the card is not in it.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_card_without_a_sentence_is_not_studied`.

### AC-03

Given a due card, when the learner answers "good", then it leaves the session and is due later than today.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_a_good_answer_takes_the_card_out_of_the_session_and_makes_it_due_later_than_today`.

### AC-04

Given a card with a wait of 8 days, when the learner answers "hard", "good" or "easy", then the card waits 10, 16 or 21 days, and each wait is longer than the one before it and none passes 365 days.

Test: `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_the_waits_of_a_card_that_has_one_follow_the_rule_of_the_decision`, `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_each_answer_but_again_waits_longer_than_the_one_before_it_and_than_the_card_did` and `backend/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_the_wait_never_goes_past_the_longest_interval`.

### AC-05

Given a due card, when the learner answers "again", then it is still due today and comes back in the session.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_an_again_answer_leaves_the_card_due_today_so_it_comes_back_in_the_session`.

### AC-06

Given answers that were given, when the product restarts, then they are still there.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_the_answers_are_still_there_after_a_restart`.

### AC-07

Given a due card, when the session offers it, then it says for each of the four answers how many days the card would wait and the day it would come back, and the answer then gives the card that day.

Test: `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_each_card_of_the_session_says_when_it_comes_back_for_each_of_the_four_answers` and `backend/tests/test_study.py::test_each_answer_gives_the_card_the_due_date_that_its_button_said`.
