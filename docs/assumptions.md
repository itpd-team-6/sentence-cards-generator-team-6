# Assumptions

## ASM-01

A free or cheap language model writes good enough sentences in Russian, English and German that regenerating is the exception, not the rule.

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** Week 2: generate sentences for 20 words in each language with one free model and count how many we would correct.

## ASM-02

Learners will review the generated sentences instead of accepting all of them unchecked.

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** Usability tests (protocol in Week 7, results in Week 9): observe whether participants use edit, flag and regenerate.

## ASM-03

Teachers want to correct the sentences in their students' cards (based on the kickoff conversation).

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** Week 2: ask one language teacher how her students study vocabulary and whether she checks it.

## ASM-04

Anki's manual "Reposition" is too slow or unknown for most learners to serve GAP-02.

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** Week 2: time how long it takes to move the words of one text to the front of an Anki deck.

## ASM-05

Learners will accept reviewing inside our app instead of in Anki (based on the kickoff conversation).

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** Week 2: look for reports in Anki community forums from learners who moved their reviews to other apps, and why. Usability tests (Week 7–9): observe whether participants are willing to review inside our app.
