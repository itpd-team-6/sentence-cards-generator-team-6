# Threshold of success

## TOS-01

The Customer accepts a working version that takes a learner from a pasted text to studying generated cards, by the end of Week 9.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** the Customer's recorded acceptance of `US-01`, `US-02` and `US-03`, each as a `DEC-nnn`, by the end of Week 9. `US-02` and `US-03` are accepted in [`DEC-011`](decisions.md#dec-011), and `US-01` stays open in [`DEC-012`](decisions.md#dec-012).

## TOS-02

A person outside the team installs and runs the product by following only `docs/setup.md`, by the end of Week 9.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** one person outside the team, in Week 9, who gets the product running from a clean checkout by following only [`setup.md`](setup.md), with the day, what they did and where they got stuck written down in the repository.

## TOS-03

A person outside the team uses the product from a pasted text to studying the cards, by the end of Week 9.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** one person outside the team, in Week 9, who pastes a text, marks words, gets the cards and studies them, with the day, what they did and where they got stuck written down in the repository.

## TOS-04

The core features are demonstrated in the working application, with no mockups, at the final project defense.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** the demonstration of the product at the final project defense, in Week 11, run on the working application and covering `US-01`, `US-02` and `US-03`.

## Failure And Success Statements

| Goal              | Success statement                                                                                           | Failure statement                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [TOS-01](#tos-01) | The Customer accepts a working version that takes a learner from a pasted text to studying generated cards. | The project failed if, by the last week, the Customer has not accepted a working version that takes a learner from a pasted text to studying generated cards. |
| [TOS-01](#tos-01) | The Customer accepts the product as the final result of the semester.                                       | The Customer did not accept the product as the final result of the semester.                                                                                  |
| [TOS-01](#tos-01) | Users can review and edit generated sentence cards before studying them.                                    | I would consider the project unsuccessful if, by the final week, users cannot review and edit generated sentence cards before studying them.                  |

| [TOS-03](#tos-03) | A person outside the team uses the product from a pasted text to studying the cards.                        | No person outside the team managed to use the product from a pasted text to studying the cards.                                                               |
| [TOS-04](#tos-04) | The core features are demonstrated in a working application, without mockups.                               | I would also consider it unsuccessful if the core features cannot be demonstrated in a working application without relying on mockups.                        |
