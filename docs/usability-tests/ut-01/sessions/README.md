# UT-01 Sessions

How a session runs, the sheet the observer fills in, and the template of the note of a counted session.
The tasks and the protocol are in [the record](../README.md), and the questions are in [the surveys](../surveys.md).

## Before a session

1. Give the participant the next free code, `P-01`, `P-02` and so on.
2. Check that the participant has consented, including to the recording.
3. Open a new library on the staging deployment, and start the recording.
4. Have the text of the participant's language ready.

## Texts for the main task

The participant uses the text in the language they study.

### English

Last weekend my friend and I took a train to a small town by a lake.
The weather was cold, but the sky was clear, so we decided to walk along the shore.
We found a tiny café, where an old man sold warm bread and strong tea.
He told us that the lake freezes every winter, and children skate on it after school.
In the evening we missed the last bus and had to wait for an hour at the station.
Still, it was one of the best trips of the year.

### German

Am Wochenende sind mein Freund und ich mit dem Zug in eine kleine Stadt am See gefahren.
Das Wetter war kalt, aber der Himmel war klar, deshalb sind wir am Ufer spazieren gegangen.
Wir haben ein winziges Café gefunden, in dem ein alter Mann warmes Brot und starken Tee verkaufte.
Er hat uns erzählt, dass der See jeden Winter zufriert und die Kinder nach der Schule darauf Schlittschuh laufen.
Am Abend haben wir den letzten Bus verpasst und mussten eine Stunde am Bahnhof warten.
Trotzdem war es einer der schönsten Ausflüge des Jahres.

### Russian

В выходные мы с другом поехали на поезде в маленький городок на берегу озера.
Погода была холодной, но небо было ясным, поэтому мы решили погулять по берегу.
Мы нашли крошечное кафе, где старик продавал тёплый хлеб и крепкий чай.
Он рассказал нам, что озеро замерзает каждую зиму, и дети катаются на нём на коньках после школы.
Вечером мы опоздали на последний автобус и целый час ждали на вокзале.
Всё равно это была одна из лучших поездок в этом году.

## Instructions for the facilitator

1. Welcome the participant, say that the product is being tested and not the participant, and check the consent and the recording.
2. Ask the pre-test questions.
3. Ask the participant to think aloud.
4. Read each scenario of the record as written, the main task first, and give the participant the text for the main task.
5. Do not explain the interface or solve a step for the participant.
6. After each task, ask the SEQ. After the three tasks, ask the SUS and the post-test.
7. Thank the participant.

## Template of the note

The note of a counted session is `sessions/P-nn.md`, with no name, email address or phone number.

```markdown
# P-01

- **Language studied:** <language>.
- **Device:** <the device>.
- **Outcome of each task, help given and time on task:** <summary>.
- **SEQ:** main <n>, secondary <n>, secondary <n>.
- **SUS:** <score>.
- **Themes:** <the themes of the notes>.
- **Quote:** "<an anonymized quote>", only if the participant agreed to it.
```
