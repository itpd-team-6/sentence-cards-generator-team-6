# Consent To Take Part In A Usability Test

The team asks every participant for this consent before the session, and a session without it does not count.
The participant gives it by replying to a message or an email that carries the text below.

- I am 18 or older.
- I agree to take part in a usability session for the sentence cards generator, and I understand that I am testing the product and not being tested.
- I agree to the session being recorded, and I understand that the recording is private.
- I agree that the sanitized results of the session may be published in the team's public repository.
- Optional: I agree that an anonymized quote from the session may be published. I still take part if I do not agree to this.
- I agree that the consent record, the link between my participant code and my name, and the raw notes are kept in the team's private usability data repository, and that the recording is kept in the team's private cloud folder.
- I agree that the course instructors can access that repository and that folder.
- I understand that the team may use a survey form and a recorder, and that their copies are deleted after the course is graded.
- I understand that the recording, the raw notes and the link between my participant code and my name are deleted after the course is graded.
- I understand that I may withdraw at any time, and that the team then removes my raw data.
