# ADR-005: Run The Audio Model In The Backend

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Created:** 2026-10-25
- **Updated:** 2026-10-25
- **Containers:** `backend`
- **Addresses:** [`QR-03`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-03.md)
- **Decision:** [`DEC-041`](../../decisions.md#dec-041)

## Context

Each card is to have audio for its word and its sentence from an outside source ([`DEC-007`](../../decisions.md#dec-007), [`DEC-010`](../../decisions.md#dec-010)), and the product must be installable by a person outside the team from `docs/setup.md` alone.
The [audio options](../../research/audio-options.md) compare three sources: Piper, which runs on the learner's machine and whose engine is GPL-3.0 with a licence for each voice, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, an online service that needs billing and receives every word and sentence, and Coqui XTTS, whose model licence allows only non-commercial use.
At the Week 4 meeting the Customer said that for a learner who does not control the machine, deploying a model on the back end that generates audio is the more user-friendly option, that Piper is the closest of the three, and that the licence must be checked for commercial use ([`DEC-041`](../../decisions.md#dec-041)).
No audio is built yet, and no functional requirement for it is written, so this record answers a quality requirement and not a behavior.

## Considered Options

- **A text-to-speech model that runs inside the `backend`.**
  The `backend` makes the audio and the `frontend` plays it, so a learner of a hosted service gets audio without running a model, and a person who hosts the product gets it with the `backend`.
- **A text-to-speech program that the learner runs apart from the product, which the product is configured to call.**
  It suits a learner on their own machine, but a learner of a hosted service would have to run it, which the Customer judged less user-friendly.
- **An online text-to-speech service that the `backend` calls.**
  It stays possible under [`DEC-010`](../../decisions.md#dec-010), but it needs a billing account, sends every word and sentence to a third party, and the Customer did not choose it.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: a text-to-speech model that runs inside the `backend`, as the Customer proposed in [`DEC-041`](../../decisions.md#dec-041).
Which model is not decided: Piper is the closest, and its licence and the licence of each voice must be checked for commercial use before it is chosen.
The record stays `Proposed` until the model and its licence are settled.

## Consequences

- **Flexibility (Installability):** the `backend` must install and hold a model, so a person who hosts the product follows more steps and needs more machine, and `docs/setup.md` must say how, or [`QR-03`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-03.md) is not met.
- **Cost:** the hardware that the model needs is not known yet; the pages read for the audio options state no minimum.
- **Cost:** the licence of the engine and of each voice may not allow commercial use, which the Customer asked to check.
- **Cost:** when the audio is built, the `backend` gains a responsibility and a functional requirement is written, and the container table and this record change in the same change.
