## Summary

- The Customer accepted `US-02` and `US-03`, and did not accept `US-01` because `AC-03` failed: he wants the sentence request repeated automatically up to a limit, a blank card when it still fails, and one message instead of one per word.
- How a card is identified changed: a word plus a short phrase in the same language with the same meaning, not the word plus its translation. The translation follows the sentence, and the learner may edit or lock it.
- The review screen and the scheduler will change: four answer buttons that show when the card returns, front and back kept apart, audio on both sides, daily limits, three classes of cards, and a way to ask for cards ahead of schedule.
- The product grows in ways that are not stories yet: decks that are separate from texts, cards of several words (a German noun with its article), selecting, hiding and deleting several cards at once, and a study queue ordered by due date.
- The next stories are `US-04`, then `US-07`, then `US-05`, then `US-06`; the audio may come from any outside source; and decisions may be made in the chat if they are recorded ([02:34:43]).

## Decisions

- [DEC-010: The audio for a word or a sentence may come from any outside source, a self-hosted text-to-speech model or an online service, and the product is configured to connect to that source and ask it for the audio.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-010)
- [DEC-011: The Customer accepted `US-02` and `US-03`.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-011)
- [DEC-012: The Customer did not accept `US-01`: `AC-03` failed, and the story stays open until the next meeting.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-012)
- [DEC-013: A card is identified by its word and a short phrase, three or four words, that uses the word with the same meaning, and the product creates no second card with the same identifier.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-013)
- [DEC-014: The translation follows the sentence, it is made again when the sentence changes, and the learner may edit it or lock it so that it stays until the learner edits the sentence again.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-014)
- [DEC-015: A flag marks a card that needs another look: the product flags a card with an invalid sentence by itself, the learner may flag any card, and the teacher sees all the cards of a student with the flagged ones first.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-015)
- [DEC-016: The cards table lets the learner select several cards to regenerate or delete in one action, asks for confirmation before deleting, and has a hide button that keeps a card in its place in the table but out of study.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-016)
- [DEC-017: The buttons of the cards table become small icons explained by a legend that stays visible while the table scrolls, the flag is renamed "mark for review", and the edit button opens a larger editor for the information that is not in the table.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-017)
- [DEC-018: The review screen has four answer buttons that each show when the card will come back, keeps the front and the back apart, plays the audio on both sides, and shows the word as it is written in the text.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-018)
- [DEC-019: The learner sets a limit of new cards and a limit of cards to review for each day, the review counts three classes of cards (new, learning and to review), and the learner can ask for cards ahead of their schedule.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-019)
- [DEC-020: A deck is separate from the texts: a card may belong to several decks if their pairs of languages are compatible, a deck may take cards from any number of texts, and the learner picks the decks when asking for sentences.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-020)
- [DEC-021: The study queue is ordered by the date each card is due, and when the learner moves cards, the product gives them a due time that keeps that order; cards never studied are an exception to the daily limit.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-021)
- [DEC-022: A card may have more than one word, a German noun with its article or a phrase, and the learner can select several words of the text as one card.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-022)
- [DEC-023: The next stories are built in this order: `US-04`, then `US-07`, then `US-05`, then `US-06`.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-023)
