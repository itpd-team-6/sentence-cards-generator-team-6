# Week 03 report

## Project

Sentence cards generator, team 6.
Assignment 3, Planning And The Minimum Usable Product: the threshold of success, the plan, the risks, the working slice, and the first release.

Our problem-space sentence: language learners (and their teachers) who study Russian, English or German from real texts want to turn the words they choose into flashcards with example sentences, translations and pronunciation, and review them with spaced repetition, without building each card by hand.

Start with the [meeting report](meeting-report.md) and the [project plan](../../docs/project-plan.md).

## What we did

We set the threshold of success, wrote the plan for the seven sprints and the register of the risks, decided the stack, and wrote the workflow documents.
We built the minimum usable product candidate into `main`: a learner pastes a text, marks the words they do not know, gets a card with a sentence and a translation for each word, checks and edits the cards in a table, and studies them.
We published the documentation site, followed `docs/setup.md` step by step and recorded the result in [issue #67](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/67), showed the working product to the Customer on 2026-10-17, and cut the first release, `REL-03`, as [v0.1.0](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/releases/tag/v0.1.0).

## Findings

- The Customer accepted `US-02` and `US-03` and did not accept `US-01`, because `AC-03` failed: he wants the request for a sentence repeated automatically up to a limit ([`DEC-011`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-011), [`DEC-012`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-012)).
- The Customer asked for changes to the product, from how a card is identified to the four answer buttons and the decks ([`DEC-013`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-013) to [`DEC-022`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-022)), and set the order of the next stories ([`DEC-023`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-023)).
- The free language model refused a request from a Russian network while we followed `docs/setup.md` ([issue #67](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/67)), which is why [`RISK-01`](../../docs/risks.md#risk-01) is the first risk of the register.

Still open: the retry of a failed sentence for `US-01`, the source of the audio, and how the Week 3 changes are spread over the sprints, which the team does at its first weekly review.
