## What we did and checked ourselves

- **We decided the threshold of success.** The team chose its goals and which failure statements to keep; the statements of AbdelrahmanAbdel-Aal and emapfff are in their own words, and Claude helped to draft the other options and the wording of the file.
- **We ran the meeting.** nataliamotand ran the validation meeting with the Customer and showed the working product against the acceptance criteria.
- **We read and ran what was built.** Every file was read line by line before it went into the repository, the product was run, and `docs/setup.md` was followed step by step; the criteria of the task issues were verified before the pull requests were approved.
- **We reviewed each other's pull requests.** The reviews are listed in the [contribution table](README.md#contribution).
- **We decided what the meeting settled.** The decisions of the Customer in [`docs/decisions.md`](../../docs/decisions.md) are worded from what he said, with the time of each quote.
