# Second Anki "Reposition" timing

Checks [`ASM-04`](../../../docs/assumptions.md#asm-04). Done by AbdelrahmanAbdel-Aal, before the Week 3 meeting.
The first measurement is in the [Week 1 checks](../../week-02/evidence/week-1-checks.md#anki-reposition-timing).

- **What was timed:** moving 20 Russian–English cards to the front of the deck in Anki.
  The cards came from a Russian text of about 100 to 150 words and were created beforehand.
  The timing started when "Cards", then "Reposition", was opened in the "Browse" window with the 20 cards filtered and selected, and stopped when the move had finished.
  The start position was set to 1.
- **What was not timed:** creating or importing the cards, searching for them and selecting them.
- **Result:** about 21 seconds, one measurement.
- **What it does and does not show:**
  - The first measurement (55 seconds) included creating the cards, and this one does not, so the two are not the same measurement and the 55 seconds cannot be compared with the 21 seconds directly.
  - It is one measurement on one text, so we cannot say how it varies.
  - Whether about 21 seconds is acceptable is an open question in the [Week 3 meeting report](../meeting-report.md#open-questions), so `ASM-04` stays `Open`.
