## Usability test

The design is in [the `UT-01` record](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md): it asks whether a learner who has not used the product before gets from a newly opened library to the first answered card without help.

The tasks are the same for every participant and run in a fixed order, with no label and no step in their wording.
In the main task the participant finds a short text on the Internet and pastes it, gets ready to practise some of its words as cards, and answers the first card.
In the second task the participant starts to practise only one group of cards in two different ways, and in the third task gets a different sentence in at least two places of the app, as [`DEC-073`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-073) records.
The criteria and the time limits are in [the tasks](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#the-tasks), and the pre-test, the SEQ after each task, the ten items of the SUS and the open-ended post-test are in [the surveys](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/surveys.md).

The recruitment target is at least three participants, with five as the design target.
A participant is 18 or older, studies Russian, English or German, has not used the product before, and is not a team member, the Customer, an instructor or course staff; the profile is in [the record](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile).
The team asks people it knows outside the team by a private message, and arranges the sessions in `SPRINT-08`; the plan and its schedule are in [the record](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#schedule).

The test's instrumentation reuses the events `text_saved`, `cards_generated`, `card_answered` and `sentence_replaced`, and adds the source `usability-test` and the participant code `P-nn` to them.
[The record's instrumentation section](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#instrumentation) says what the test observes and what the events cannot cover, which the observation records.
On 2026-11-15 `nataliamotand` ran a test session on staging with the code `P-99` and read its four events from the analytics backend; the query, the rows and what was not checked are in [the verification record](instrumentation-verification.md).

The consent records, the identity mapping, the raw notes and the filtered export of the instrumentation will be in the [private usability data repository](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6-usability-data), and the recordings in the team's cloud folder, whose link is in the Moodle submission.
The repository link is excluded from the link check in [.lycheeignore](../../.lycheeignore), because the repository needs authentication and the link was opened by hand, signed in, on 2026-11-14.
The Customer can open the private repository and the recordings folder.
The public record is the test record, the surveys, the consent template, the session notes and the results, and it carries no participant identity.

[`QR-08`](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-08.md) is the `Interaction Capability` and `Self-descriptiveness` requirement, and its manual test [`QRT-01`](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-08.md#qrt-01) names `UT-01`; it reads the median SEQ of the main task, against a bar of at least 5 of 7.

The Week 8 handoff is the design, the recruitment target of at least three participants with five as the design target, the profile, the plan to arrange the sessions, the private repository and the recordings folder, the instrumentation and its verification, the `QR-08 QRT-01` row in `SPRINT-08`, the `results.md` stub and the analysis and triage plan.
