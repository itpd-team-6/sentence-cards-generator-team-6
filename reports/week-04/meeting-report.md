## Summary

- The Customer accepted `US-01`, and did not accept `US-04` yet: `AC-02` and `AC-03` are to be rewritten so that the interval and the refused order are clear, and deleting a card has to remove it from the study queue at once. He confirmed both verdicts in the chat after the meeting ("Confirmed", 2026-10-24 00:00, [the chat evidence](evidence/customer-chat-confirmation.md)).
- The quality requirements change: a new one says that no user sees the key of another user, the time requirement measures the time to show the cards after the model answers and not the time of the model, and the key requirement covers the logs too.
- The architecture changes in two places: a user who does not use the key of the service keeps their own key in the browser and the browser asks the language model, and the audio model runs in the back end.
- The product gains checks and fixes before the next stories: a minimum and a maximum length for each sentence, words sent in batches that arrive independently, "Due today" and "Later" fixed with separate scrolls, and a repeat time that can be configured.
- The next stories are audio, then the daily limits with asking for more cards, then the decks apart from the texts, then the teacher's review; a card stays identified by its word and a short phrase, but the question to the language model is to be revised, and what happens when it misjudges is not decided.

## Decisions

- [DEC-030: The Customer accepted `US-01`, so the story is closed.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-030)
- [DEC-031: The Customer did not accept `US-04` yet: `AC-02` and `AC-03` are to be rewritten so that they are clear, and the story stays open.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-031)
- [DEC-032: Deleting a card removes it from the study queue at once, so that the cards table and the study queue show the same state.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-032)
- [DEC-033: The product checks the length of each sentence that the language model writes against a minimum and a maximum, counts a sentence outside the limits as a missing one, and asks the model again by the rule of `DEC-024`; the team chooses the two limits.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-033)
- [DEC-034: The product sends the words of a text to the language model in several batches that are generated independently of each other; the cards of one batch appear together when the batch is done, and the product does not wait for the other batches.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-034)
- [DEC-035: In the study queue the headings "Due today" and "Later" stay in view, and each of the two lists has its own scroll.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-035)
- [DEC-036: The time that the product spends repeating a request to the language model is configurable, and its default is 20 seconds.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-036)
- [DEC-037: The learner edits a sentence or a translation in a modal, and editing in place by double-clicking is not built.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-037)
- [DEC-038: The product has a quality requirement that no user can see the key of another user, so that a teacher never sees the key of a student.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-038)
- [DEC-039: The product does not measure the time that the language model takes as a quality of the product; it measures the time that the product takes to show the cards after the answer of the model has arrived.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-039)
- [DEC-040: A user who does not use the built-in key may keep their own key in the browser, the browser then makes the requests to the language model, and that key never goes to the server.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-040)
- [DEC-041: The model that makes the audio runs in the back end, so that a learner of a hosted service gets audio without running a model; Piper is the closest of the three options, and the licence of each option is to be checked for commercial use.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-041)
- [DEC-042: The next stories are built in this order: the audio of words and sentences, then the daily limits with asking for more cards, then the decks apart from the texts, then the teacher's review (`US-05`); `US-06` is not in this order and its place is not decided.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-042)
- [DEC-043: A card is still identified by its word and a short phrase (`DEC-013`); the team revises the prompt that asks the language model whether to make a new card, tries longer context phrases, and tries the format that the Customer proposed.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-043)
