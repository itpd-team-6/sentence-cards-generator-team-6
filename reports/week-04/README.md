# Week 04 report

## Project

Sentence cards generator, team 6.
Assignment 4, Quality, Requirements, Architecture, And The Fourth Release: the quality and functional requirements, the architecture, the sprint plan and the first retrospective, and the second release.

Our problem-space sentence: language learners (and their teachers) who study Russian, English or German from real texts want to turn the words they choose into flashcards with example sentences, translations and pronunciation, and review them with spaced repetition, without building each card by hand.

Start with the [meeting report](meeting-report.md) and the [project plan](../../docs/project-plan.md#sprint-04).

## What we did

We wrote the quality requirements, the functional requirements, the container view and the architectural decision records, and the glossary and the instructions for agents.
We planned `SPRINT-04`, estimated the stories, and brought the issues of Weeks 2 and 3 to the Week 4 shape in one pass.
We built the changes the Customer asked for at the Week 3 meeting, ran the product against the acceptance criteria of `US-01` and `US-04` in the validation meeting of 2026-10-23, and recorded what the Customer decided.
We cut the second release, `REL-04`, as [v0.2.0](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/releases/tag/v0.2.0).
`US-04` did not finish, and it moved to `SPRINT-05`.

## Findings

- The Customer accepted `US-01` and did not accept `US-04` yet: its `AC-02` and `AC-03` are to be rewritten so that they are clear, and deleting a card has to remove it from the study queue at once ([`DEC-030`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-030), [`DEC-031`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-031), [`DEC-032`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-032)).
- The quality requirements and the architecture changed after the meeting: a new requirement that no user sees the key of another user, a time requirement that measures the product and not the language model, a user's own key kept in the browser, and an audio model that runs in the back end ([`DEC-038`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-038) to [`DEC-041`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-041)).
- The Customer set the order of the next stories: audio, then the daily limits, then the decks, then the teacher's review ([`DEC-042`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-042)).

Still open: the rewrite of `US-04`, the choice and licence of the audio model, the revised prompt that decides whether a new card is made ([`DEC-043`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-043)), and what happens when the language model misjudges. The open questions are in the [meeting report](meeting-report.md#open-questions).
