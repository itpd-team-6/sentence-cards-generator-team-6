# Week 05 report

## Project

Sentence cards generator, team 6.
Assignment 5, Deployment, Tests, Continuous Integration And The Fifth Release: the product on the university virtual machine, the tests of the quality requirements and their plan, the product continuous integration, the feature documentation, the validation meeting and the third release of the product.

Our problem-space sentence: language learners (and their teachers) who study Russian, English or German from real texts want to turn the words they choose into flashcards with example sentences, translations and pronunciation, and review them with spaced repetition, without building each card by hand.

The testing plan is in [docs/project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan](../../docs/project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan), and the rows that are not `Passes` are only there.

Start with the [meeting report](meeting-report.md), the [project plan](../../docs/project-plan.md#sprint-05) and the [deployment verification](deployment-verification.md).

## What we did

We deployed the product by hand to the university virtual machine, on two ports: a staging instance on port `8001` and a production instance on port `8002`, both running the commit `e749a2b` as systemd services, each with its own configuration, database and secrets ([`docs/deployment.md`](../../docs/deployment.md)).
The core task was verified on production from a campus machine, and the Customer opened the product himself from the university network at the validation meeting ([deployment verification](deployment-verification.md)).
We defined two milestones: [`MLS-05`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-05), due 2026-10-31, which delivers the study of the words of one text first and the product opened in a browser on the virtual machine without installing anything, and [`MLS-06`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-06), due 2026-11-07, which is planned for `SPRINT-06`.
We added continuous integration for the product code and for the workflow files (`actionlint`), wrote the tests of the quality requirements, documented the features in [`docs/features.md`](../../docs/features.md), and made deleting a card remove it from the study queue at once.
We cut the third release, `REL-05`, as [v0.2.1](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/releases/tag/v0.2.1), and the Customer accepted `US-04` and `US-09`.

The product continuous integration has a named step for each check that applies to the stack: lint and format check on the front end and on the back end, the tests of both, and the build of the front end.
Three checks do not apply, and the [workflow](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml) records each with its reason: type checking on the front end, because it is JavaScript and not TypeScript; type checking on the back end, because it has no type checker configured; and the build of the back end, because it is Python run from its source.
The workflow has no deployment job, because it cannot reach the university network.

What moved out of the sprint: the revision of the question to the model ([issue #165](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/165)), which moved to `SPRINT-06`.
No story moved out of the sprint: `US-04` and `US-09` were accepted.

## Findings

- The Customer accepted `US-04` and `US-09`, and the two stories are closed ([`DEC-045`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-045), [`DEC-053`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-053)).
  `US-09` got a fourth acceptance criterion about the browsers ([`DEC-052`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-052)).
- He accepted the language model of the virtual machine, Mistral Small 3.1 from Cloudflare Workers AI ([`DEC-046`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-046)).
- The order of the next stories changed: the decks, then the audio, then the daily limits ([`DEC-047`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-047)).
  The study queue gets a list of decks and two tabs ([`DEC-048`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-048)), and a button that regenerates empty or invalid cards ([`DEC-049`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-049)).
- The teacher's side of the product is dropped, and an offline mobile version takes its place ([`DEC-050`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-050)).
- After the meeting the Customer asked in the chat that the selection editor keep the line breaks of the pasted text ([`DEC-054`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-054)).

Still open: the revision of the question to the model, the scenarios for the due date and the time zone, the decks list and its two tabs, what happens when the model misjudges a card, how the re-ordering of the queue works with the daily limits, and what the offline mobile version covers.
The open questions are in the [meeting report](meeting-report.md#open-questions).
