## Summary

- The Customer accepted `US-04` ("I think we can finalize this user story.", [00:22:28]). For `US-09` he accepted `AC-01` and `AC-02` and called `AC-03` "more technical than necessary", but fine to keep; he asked for a criterion about browsers, which the team added after the meeting, tested in Firefox and Chrome on a computer and in Chrome on a phone, and sent to him in the chat, where he answered "Also works in Firefox on mobile phone. Yes, it's done" (Customer, chat, 2026-10-31 17:20), so `US-09` is closed ([`DEC-053`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-053)).
- The Customer accepted the language model of the virtual machine, Mistral Small 3.1 from Cloudflare Workers AI, and expects that a user can later bring their own provider and key.
- The order of the next stories changed: the decks come first, then the audio, then the daily limits with asking for more cards. The teacher's side of the product is dropped, and an offline mobile version, released as an installable file on GitHub, takes its place.
- The decks in the study queue are a list of decks with checkboxes and two tabs, and a button that regenerates empty or invalid cards replaces the second flag.
- The time zone is tied to the daily limits, and how the due date is stored and shown is not settled: the team will bring scenarios for the Customer to see.

## Decisions

- [DEC-045: The Customer accepted `US-04`, so the story is closed.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-045)
- [DEC-046: The language model of the virtual machine is Mistral Small 3.1 from Cloudflare Workers AI, and the Customer expects that a user can later choose another provider with their own key.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-046)
- [DEC-047: The next stories are built in this order: the decks apart from the texts, then the audio of words and sentences, then the daily limits with asking for more cards; the teacher's review is not in the order.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-047)
- [DEC-048: The study queue gets a list of decks above the cards, a checkbox for each deck, a "select all" button with two states, and two tabs, one to choose which decks to study and one to re-order the cards; the queue shows only the cards of the selected decks.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-048)
- [DEC-049: The product has a button that regenerates the cards that are empty or invalid, in place of a second flag "flag to regenerate".](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-049)
- [DEC-050: The product does not build the teacher's side, so the teacher's review (`US-05`) is not built, and its place goes to a mobile version of the product that works offline, released as an installable file on GitHub for the first version.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-050)
- [DEC-051: The voices of the audio need no licence for commercial use, and they must be compatible with the MIT licence of the product.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-051)
- [DEC-052: `US-09` gets a fourth acceptance criterion about the browsers in which the product opens and works.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-052)
- [DEC-053: The Customer accepted `US-09` with its four acceptance criteria, so the story is closed.](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-053)
