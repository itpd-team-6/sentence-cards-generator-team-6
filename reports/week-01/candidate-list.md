# Candidate List – Week 01

List of tools people use to create language flashcards.

| #   | Tool        | Link                                  | Why relevant                                                     | Selected?                    | Reason if not selected                                           |
| --- | ----------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Anki        | <https://apps.ankiweb.net/>           | Most popular open-source spaced repetition system                | Yes (as ALT-04 with ChatGPT) | -                                                                |
| 2   | Quizlet     | <https://quizlet.com/>                | Very popular, has AI card generation and classes for teachers    | Yes (ALT-03)                 | -                                                                |
| 3   | songs2anki  | <https://github.com/deemp/songs2anki> | Official proof-of-concept mentioned in the course                | Yes (ALT-01)                 | -                                                                |
| 4   | LingQ       | <https://www.lingq.com/>              | Learns vocabulary from real texts + has flashcards               | Yes (ALT-02)                 | -                                                                |
| 5   | RemNote     | <https://www.remnote.com/>            | Notes + AI flashcards                                            | No                           | More note-taking focused than pure language sentence cards       |
| 6   | Memrise     | <https://www.memrise.com/>            | Language learning with vocabulary                                | No                           | More course-based, less flexible card creation                   |
| 7   | Brainscape  | <https://www.brainscape.com/>         | Adaptive flashcards                                              | No                           | Less focus on sentence context                                   |
| 8   | Clozemaster | <https://www.clozemaster.com/>        | Sentences in context                                             | No                           | Good for sentences but not easy card generation from custom text |
| 9   | Mochi       | <https://mochi.cards/>                | Flashcards + notes                                               | No                           | Smaller user base, less AI features                              |
| 10  | Knowt       | <https://knowt.com/>                  | AI study tools                                                   | No                           | More general study, not specialized in language sentences        |
| 11  | WaniKani    | <https://www.wanikani.com/>           | Japanese only                                                    | No                           | Only Japanese, not multi-language                                |
| 12  | Lingvist    | <https://lingvist.com/>               | Personalized vocabulary                                          | No                           | Closed system, hard to generate custom cards                     |
| 13  | Readlang    | <https://readlang.com/>               | Similar to LingQ – reads real texts and creates vocabulary cards | No                           | Very close to LingQ, already covered by ALT-02                   |
