## Anki "Reposition" timing

Checks [`ASM-04`](../../../docs/assumptions.md#asm-04). Done by AbdelrahmanAbdel-Aal.

- **What was timed:** taking the words of a short German text ("Das Haus ist groß", written by ChatGPT), creating the cards in Anki, and moving them to the front of the deck.
- **Result:** 55 seconds, one measurement.
- **What it does and does not show:**
  - The time includes making the cards, not only moving them, so it overstates the cost of "Reposition" alone.
  - The text is a few words long, so it understates the cost for a real text.
  - With one measurement we cannot say whether this is slow, so `ASM-04` stays `Open`.
- **Next:** a second measurement with a realistic text and only the move timed (action point in the [Week 2 meeting report](../meeting-report.md#action-points)).

## Forum search

Checks [`ASM-05`](../../../docs/assumptions.md#asm-05). Done by emapfff. The summary was checked against the three threads afterwards (see `ai-usage.md`).

- **Anki forums:** no thread was found where learners moved their reviews to another app. The thread read is about onboarding and first setup, and one user there moved from Quizlet to Anki ([the thread](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/revisiting-onboarding-decks-and-tutorials/43692?page=4)).
- **LingQ forums:** several learners dropped or cut down Anki because the daily reviews took too much time, piled up or got boring; some now use LingQ's own review or only read and listen ([thread 1](https://forum.lingq.com/t/is-anki-a-necessity/36860), [thread 2](https://forum.lingq.com/t/use-anki-or-not/380455)).
- **What it shows:** learners leave Anki because of the review load, not because they want to review somewhere else. That is weak evidence for `ASM-05`, so it stays `Open`.
- **A question it suggests:** is keeping the daily review small as important to the Customer as having the review inside our app?
