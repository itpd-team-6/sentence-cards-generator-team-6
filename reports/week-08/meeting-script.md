# Week 8 Sprint Review script

## Context

The product helps a learner who reads a text in a language they study to remember its words: the learner marks words, and the product makes cards for them.
The team believes that `UT-01` ran as designed and that its four sessions meet `QR-08`, that the participants met problems that the team must turn into tasks, and that `US-20` and `US-16` do what their acceptance criteria say.
The meeting has to settle whether the four sessions count and `QR-08` and the threshold of success `TOS-03` are met, whether the findings and their severities are right, and whether `US-20` and `US-16` are accepted.

## Agenda

1. **Permission questions (1 minute).**
   - **Show:** nothing.
   - The moderator asks whether the team may record, whether it may publish a sanitized transcript in the repository, and whether it may share the transcript privately with the instructors if publication is refused.
2. **The Week 7 action points (4 minutes).**
   - **Show:** nothing; the moderator gives the status of each action point of the [Week 7 report](../week-07/meeting-report.md#action-points).
   - **Questions:** 1.
3. **The results of `UT-01` and the verdicts (10 minutes).**
   - **Show:** the table of the four sessions, with each task's outcome and the SEQ, shared on screen; [the record](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md), [`QR-08`](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-08.md) and the threshold of success `TOS-03`.
   - **Questions:** 2, 3 and 4.
4. **The findings (25 minutes).**
   - **Show:** the findings one at a time, each with its severity and the page of the product where it happened.
   - **Questions:** 5, 6 and 7.
5. **The stories `US-20` and `US-16` (15 minutes).**
   - **Show:** the staging deployment; each story, [US-20](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/253) and [US-16](https://github.com/itpd-team-6/sentence-cards-generator-team-6/issues/233), is run against its acceptance criteria, one at a time.
   - **Questions:** 8.
6. **Read-back (5 minutes).**
   - **Show:** nothing.
   - The moderator reads back the decisions and the action points.

## Questions

1. _(closed)_ Can the three action points that the team did not carry out this week, the test of the meaning records, the stories for the Texts tab and the scenarios for the time zones, move to the next sprint?
2. _(closed)_ Do the four sessions count, although parts of the recordings of three of them are missing?
3. _(closed)_ Do you consider `QR-08` met, with a median SEQ of 5 of 7 on the main task over four sessions?
4. _(closed)_ Do you agree that `TOS-03` is achieved?
5. _(open)_ What would you change in the product for each finding, and what do you think of the way we plan to change it?
6. _(closed)_ Do you agree with the severities we gave, and is there a finding that you see as minor and not a priority now?
7. _(open)_ How will the team address the new findings together with the earlier comments in the time left?
8. _(closed)_ Do you accept `US-20` and `US-16`, each against its acceptance criteria?

## Roles

- **Note taker:** nataliamotand, from the recording.
- **Observer:** None.

## Key improvements

- **Before:** "Do you agree with the findings?" It is closed and vague, and a "yes" says nothing about what the team should do first.
- **After:** "Do you agree with the severities we gave, and is there a finding that you see as minor and not a priority now?" (question 6).
- **Principle:** ask for a judgment that sets the order of the work; an answer that names a finding to postpone changes what the team builds next, and a bare agreement changes nothing.
